# Identify emission hotspots

Apply the [scope 3 and inventory contract](../../../docs/scope-3-inventory-contract.md) for method sources, common envelopes, helper signatures and limits.

Choose one contribution level from a selected inventory: scope totals, scope 3 category totals or reconciled source components. Exclude the total itself and alternative scope 2 accounts from contributions. Reconcile nonoverlap and shared-source coverage before ranking by normalized CO2e. Identify each contribution and denominator through metric/evidence lineage. Report percentages only for a supported positive denominator, disclose missing coverage and label partial-inventory shares. A hotspot ranking suggests investigation; it does not establish feasible savings, verified reduction potential or an environmental claim.

Return a common result and checked proposed state. Retain assumptions, gaps, outstanding review requirements and evidence IDs; every quantity has units, period, boundary, method, uncertainty and calculation lineage. No external publication or state-file mutation follows from invocation.
