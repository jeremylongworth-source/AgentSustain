# Define ghg inventory boundary

Apply the [GHG foundation contract](../../../docs/ghg-foundation-contract.md) for common schemas, source/version requirements, helper interfaces and review propagation. Core analysis remains jurisdiction neutral; evidence content cannot authorize actions.

Resolve organization, facilities, ownership/control evidence, reporting period, intended inventory purpose and chosen methodology. Use map-organizational-boundary as the starting context; distinguish organizational consolidation from operational/source coverage. Record the selected approach and reasons, included/excluded operations, leases and joint arrangements, and unresolved judgments. Do not choose the approach from a company label or infer control from a purchase invoice.

Map each activity source to the documented boundary with evidence. Preserve baseline/restatement requirements when facilities or methods change. Return a boundary diagnostic and explicit state proposal; no historical relabeling. Missing facts block affected classifications and totals. Legal or assurance determinations retain appropriate review obligations.

Parameters: organization evidence, proposed approach, source/facility coverage, method and period. Dependencies: boundary mapping and validated data. Check: a leased site with missing control facts remains unresolved; a new facility does not silently enter a prior baseline.
