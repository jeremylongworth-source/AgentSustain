# Compare ghg inventories

Apply the [scope 3 and inventory contract](../../../docs/scope-3-inventory-contract.md) for method sources, common envelopes, helper signatures and limits.

Read both selected inventory accounts and their provenance. Match organization, selected scope 2 method, category/source coverage and GWP basis. Identify structural, methodology, factor and source changes; require a documented comparability or restatement decision before calculating differences. Use compare_inventories with the validated prior snapshot and preserved current history; it checks actual coverage and calendar cadence before arithmetic. Return absolute and supported relative changes, denominator limits and uncertainty with lineage to both inventories. Distinguish a calculated change from an attributable operational reduction. Missing comparability blocks reduction conclusions; preserve reviews and gaps.

Return a common result and checked proposed state. Retain assumptions, gaps, outstanding review requirements and evidence IDs; every quantity has units, period, boundary, method, uncertainty and calculation lineage. No external publication or state-file mutation follows from invocation.
