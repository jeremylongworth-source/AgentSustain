# Calculate scope 3 category

Apply the [scope 3 and inventory contract](../../../docs/scope-3-inventory-contract.md) for method sources, common envelopes, helper signatures and limits.

Select one category and inspect its minimum boundary and applicable calculation chapter. Resolve activity and lifecycle factor coverage, allocation and reporting-year cohort treatment before arithmetic. Use calculate_category for supported physical-activity components, with policy and coverage records retained. Obtain a separately sourced derivation for unsupported spend, lifetime, hybrid or financial methods. Missing defensible factors produce EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED; do not fill them from memory. Preserve missing supplier data, estimates, uncertainty, omissions and open reviews. Do not net avoided emissions, offsets or recycling benefits into the inventory.

Return a common result and checked proposed state. Retain assumptions, gaps, outstanding review requirements and evidence IDs; every quantity has units, period, boundary, method, uncertainty and calculation lineage. No external publication or state-file mutation follows from invocation.
