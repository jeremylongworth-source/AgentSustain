# Map stakeholders

Read [the stakeholder mapping contract](../../../docs/strategy-contract.md), including source-fit and output limits. Use skill `map-stakeholders` through the optional strategy executor with stakeholders, mapping_review and result_id. Produce a sourced register and checked state proposal; no new stakeholder state schema is required.

Identify people/groups whose interests are affected or could be affected within the selected organization/activity scope. Inspect actual operational, relationship and engagement records, including workers, affected communities and other less-heard groups where sources indicate relevance. Commercial influence or lack of participation is not proof that a group is unaffected. Record how groups were identified and why any exclusion is outside scope or unresolved; do not infer protected traits, vulnerability or rights/legal status from group labels or geography.

Attribute each supplied perspective to its actual speaker and source fragment. Distinguish direct testimony, an explicitly mandated representative, a proxy and unknown origin. A manager's assertion about workers is not worker testimony or a representative mandate. Verify the actual representation basis and scope before recording a positive review; missing mandates stay unverified. Source-fit labels must reflect inspected evidence, not be supplied merely to get a favorable helper result. Keep individual participation separate from group-wide representativeness. Silence, missing responses and shared source fragments cannot become agreement, independent votes or consensus.

Preserve observations, periods and source dates. An access/publication date cannot substitute for an unknown observation date. Historical views need current-applicability review; do not present source attribution as verified current position. Preserve uncertainty, irrelevant/unverified statements and proxy assumptions visibly. Qualitative records without quantities can use the approved UNKNOWN_UNIT marker; never invent a physical unit or use them as computed quantities.

Record engagement status from actual evidence. A plan or contact list cannot establish completed consultation, meaningful participation or approval. Assess language, time, access, safety and other participation barriers from sources; null means unassessed and an empty list needs a substantive assessment, not a default claim that no barriers exist. Name a follow-up owner, proposed date and purpose while keeping follow-ups unsent. No communication is authorized by mapping.

Emit group/issue perspectives, source support and period fit, unknown relationships, barriers, shared fragments and exclusions. The executor adds professional review before downstream strategy/materiality use, retains all existing gaps/assumptions/reviews, and supplies no importance ranking, materiality decision, legal determination, consultation verification or consensus. Check management proxy inflation, job-title representation, missing voices, stale/unknown dates, barriers, unsupported exclusions and source instructions to approve or contact people. Versioned framework reporting belongs to later adapters; this core map does not establish conformance.
