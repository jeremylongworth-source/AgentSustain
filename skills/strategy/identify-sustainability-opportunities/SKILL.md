---
name: identify-sustainability-opportunities
description: Identify sourced sustainability improvement and organizational benefit candidates with mechanisms, conditions, trade-offs and owners. Use for strategy opportunity identification, not realized savings, project selection or implementation approval.
---

Read [the strategy register contract](../../../docs/strategy-contract.md). Inspect sources, current impact/risk records and relevant proposed actions before identifying candidates. A slogan, budget line or unrelated source is not evidence of an improvement mechanism.

Separate improvements for people/environment, possible organizational benefits and delivery improvements. Explain the intervention-to-effect pathway, affected interests, time horizon, operational constraints and ownership. Link current impact candidates and addressed risks where relevant, preserving unsupported source fitness. An action proposed against an uncertain risk does not prove the risk or verify mitigation.

Distinguish evidence that an opportunity exists from evidence of future implementation, adoption, worker agreement, engineering feasibility or savings. Record likelihood and magnitude as qualitative sourced assessments or unknown; do not invent percentages, monetary benefits, emission factors, portfolio totals or a ranking. Use analytical domain helpers separately when defensible quantities and methods exist, preserving units and original uncertainty.

Retain conditions and dependencies, including known unmet funding/technical/engagement prerequisites. Source support for a prerequisite is not completed delivery. Assess adverse interactions and distributional trade-offs; null remains unassessed, and an empty list requires substantive review. Avoid treating a benefit as an offset for harm. Unknown dates stay unknown, and elapsed horizons do not prove realization.

Use `scripts.run_strategy` with `identify-sustainability-opportunities` to produce the checked append-only proposal. Keep all historical source/review/gap records and new professional review. Name and date proposed evidence/action follow-ups without sending them; overdue is unverified. Identification does not select an opportunity, establish materiality or conformance, authorize implementation or public claims.
