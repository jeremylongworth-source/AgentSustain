---
name: identify-material-sustainability-issues
description: Identify candidate sustainability issues from sourced impacts and an explicit significance method, retaining stakeholder gaps and materiality review. Use for impact materiality screening, not financial materiality or final reporting approval.
---

Read [the strategy contract](../../../docs/strategy-contract.md) for the separate-impact threshold method and helper fields. Inspect actual source content before confirming method or impact fitness; a known source ID, popular survey item or management assertion alone is insufficient.

Establish selected scope, period, activities and business relationships, affected people and missing perspectives. Identify actual and potential positive and negative impacts; retain allegations, proxies, unknown dates, causal relationship uncertainty and omitted topics/sites. Do not treat missing records or stakeholder silence as immateriality. Link a current reproducible stakeholder map when available; it informs perspectives without proving harm, consultation, consensus or voting power.

Require a versioned sourced significance method with explicit ordinal anchors and thresholds. Withhold model fitness if its cited source lacks that method or is unsuitable for the selected scope. Assess adverse scale, scope and irremediability with substantive rationale; distinguish likelihood of a potential event from severity of an actual harm. For potential human-rights harms preserve severity precedence rather than discounting serious harm through low likelihood. Keep positive benefit scale and scope separate; never offset negative impacts with benefits, savings or remediation promises. A financial risk or NPV is not impact significance.

Use `scripts.run_strategy` with `identify-material-sustainability-issues` to generate the checked proposal. Its selected topic candidates retain significance evidence, unresolved impacts and any reproduced stakeholder perspectives. Below supplied thresholds is not a universal immateriality finding. Unknown remains unknown; qualitative ordinal labels do not become numeric physical metrics or weighted totals.

Keep owned evidence/review follow-ups proposed and unsent, all previous review requirements and a new materiality specialist gate. Do not approve final material topics, resolve rights/legal questions, claim framework conformance, authorize actions or publish. Versioned framework adapters and complete organization determinations require their own evidence and review.
