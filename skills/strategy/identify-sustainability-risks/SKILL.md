---
name: identify-sustainability-risks
description: Identify sourced sustainability risk pathways affecting people, the environment, the organization or strategy delivery, preserving uncertain effects and review ownership. Use for strategy risk identification, not specialist climate modeling or quantified loss forecasts.
---

Read [the strategy register contract](../../../docs/strategy-contract.md) for helper fields and source reproduction. Inspect actual sources before confirming a mechanism, likelihood, magnitude or dependency; known source IDs do not establish fitness.

Distinguish an adverse impact on people/environment from its possible effects on the organization and from delivery obstacles. Trace a specific dependency, activity or impact through a plausible mechanism to affected interests, operations or financial channels. Do not automatically turn a material-issue candidate into an organizational loss, legal breach or risk acceptance. A positive benefit cannot serve as adverse risk evidence. Link current material-issue records when available, retaining unresolved impacts and incomplete issue coverage.

Record source observations separately from the prospective horizon. Unknown dates remain unknown; access or receipt dates cannot substitute. Explicitly retain missing or elapsed horizons without implying that an event occurred. Source updates after the historical reporting period may inform prospective planning as of review, but do not restate past inventory or results.

Keep likelihood and magnitude qualitative with their own supporting evidence and substantive rationale. Missing assessments stay null; do not invent probabilities, default ranks, monetary losses, factors or savings. Unknowns, proxies and weak causal support remain investigation candidates. Avoid ranking or aggregating heterogeneous risks without a separately supported method.

Use `scripts.run_strategy` with `identify-sustainability-risks` for the checked proposal. Retain dependencies as supported, known unmet or unresolved with owner/source context; documented support is not completed work or verified control effectiveness. Trade-offs null means unassessed; an empty list requires an actual substantive review rather than a default. Preserve old reviews/gaps and new professional review. Owned follow-ups remain proposed and unsent, overdue dates unverified. Do not accept risk, authorize mitigation, certify compliance or make public claims.
