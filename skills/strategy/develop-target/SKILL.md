# Develop target

Read [the strategy contract](../../../docs/strategy-contract.md), especially target development and execution limits. Use the optional executor for supported absolute/physical-intensity reduction endpoints with definition_result_id, kpi_id, target, target_review and result_id. It reproduces baseline/KPI dependencies and proposes state without adopting a target.

Start with the decision maker's proposed ambition and actual source evidence. Distinguish absolute from intensity objectives, target scope from corporate footprint, a target endpoint from forecast delivery, and target commitment period from baseline period. State rationale, owner, completion/commitment dates, growth and service assumptions, exclusions, double counting and recalculation policy. Do not invent a percentage, future production, factor, emissions budget or technical/financial feasibility to fill a gap. A public, science-based or net-zero assertion requires its own versioned method and review; this core skill cannot validate it.

Inspect the baseline and denominator sources, product mix and operating context before confirming review fields. Source IDs and positive equal counts do not prove equivalent service. A scope or period change requires an explicit defensible comparable method; the initial executor cannot annualize or average a multi-year target. Its supported method excludes offsets/credits/removals and preserves gross quantities. Other target forms need an explicit method rather than relabeling a net outcome as gross.

For supplied percent reductions calculate baseline × (1 − percent/100); for an endpoint use the declared matching unit. Preserve all source lineage, uncertainty and incomplete-coverage gaps. For intensity retain the absolute baseline and leave absolute future impact unknown without a defensible future activity scenario. Do not extrapolate a straight or compound annual pathway from an endpoint.

Emit a proposed objective, endpoint metric and outstanding professional/owner review. Adoption, claims, implementation and feasibility remain unestablished; completing arithmetic does not grant any of them. No external communication follows. Check zero/unknown baseline, 0–100 percent range, exact endpoint units, incompatible periods, source changes, intensity-versus-absolute confusion, and attempted offset/net-zero claims. Later `evaluate-target-feasibility` work must link defensible initiative, dependency, investment and scenario evidence before judging delivery.
