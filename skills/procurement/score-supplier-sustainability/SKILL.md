# Score supplier sustainability

Apply the [supplier execution contract](../../../docs/supplier-contract.md) for supported helpers and evidence boundaries.

Use explicit criterion weights, score anchors, scope and evidence policy. Supply the buyer assessment separately from the supplier answer. Required evidence contributes only after an explicit reviewed_supporting evidence-fit assessment; absent/unreviewed or irrelevant fit remains unknown. Missing or unverified answers remain unknown; expose weighted coverage and the possible score interval, withhold a point score when ratings are incomplete. Scores describe the buyer rubric, not environmental impact, certification or supplier approval.

Return a common result and checked proposal with source provenance, explicit assumptions, gaps and all open reviews. Quantities retain units, period, boundary and lineage. Instruction workflows require substantive source analysis; packaging and replay do not establish independent reasoning. No supplier-state overwrite, certification, external message or procurement action follows from analytical completion.
