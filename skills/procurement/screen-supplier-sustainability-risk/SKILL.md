# Screen supplier sustainability risk

Apply the [supplier execution contract](../../../docs/supplier-contract.md) for supported helpers and evidence boundaries.

Separate documented incidents and exposure from unverified allegations and sector/geography proxies. Record source dates, affected product/site, confidence, severity basis and reviewer needs. Unknown evidence is an investigation need, not a finding of misconduct. Do not invent country or industry risk scores.

Return a common result and checked proposal with source provenance, explicit assumptions, gaps and all open reviews. Quantities retain units, period, boundary and lineage. Instruction workflows require substantive source analysis; packaging and replay do not establish independent reasoning. No supplier-state overwrite, certification, external message or procurement action follows from analytical completion.

Use `screen-supplier-sustainability-risk` for an explicit supplier, sourced screening review, declared ordinal severity/likelihood anchors and owned impact register. Describe scale, scope, irremediability, buyer relationship and source applicability separately. Severity groups contain only reviewed supporting supplier-specific impacts; allegations and sector/geography proxies remain investigation needs even when the caller supplies high ranks. Equal severities stay tied and likelihood is retained without multiplication. A source reference or filled review field does not authenticate the underlying judgment. Unknown likelihood or buyer relationship remains a gap even when a supported severity can be grouped. Keep the generated due-diligence review open; no supplier clearance, misconduct finding or legal determination follows.

Use observed_date=null when the document does not establish an observation/event date. Preserve the source access date separately; never substitute it as a known event date.
