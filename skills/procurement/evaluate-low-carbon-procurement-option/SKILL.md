# Evaluate low carbon procurement option

Apply the [supplier execution contract](../../../docs/supplier-contract.md) for supported helpers and evidence boundaries.

Compare equivalent function, lifetime and service using sourced lifecycle boundaries and defensible emissions inputs. Keep baseline and alternatives, transport/end-of-life assumptions and double-counting risks explicit. Reuse carbon helpers for factors and finance helpers for cost; never turn a marketing claim, spend or supplier corporate total into a product reduction.

Return a common result and checked proposal with source provenance, explicit assumptions, gaps and all open reviews. Quantities retain units, period, boundary and lineage. Instruction workflows require substantive source analysis; packaging and replay do not establish independent reasoning. No supplier-state overwrite, certification, external message or procurement action follows from analytical completion.

Use `evaluate-low-carbon-procurement-option` for two mutually exclusive options with selected mapping links and sourced positive equivalent-service metrics. The helper reproduces purchase mappings and checks matching service amounts, lifecycle/allocation and GWP basis; it applies no automatic mass or lifetime scaling. Retain the review of product performance, lifetime, exclusions, scenario assumptions and broader tradeoffs. Missing paired costs require null cost review and remain a gap; known quotes require matching currency, valuation, dollar/tax basis and cost scope. Existing finance-derived costs are reproduced and shadow-price ancestry rejected. Signed selected emissions/cost differences do not establish realized savings, product superiority, public claims or supplier selection.
