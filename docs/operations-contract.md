# SUS-13 sustainable operations composition

The sustainable-operations skillset composes existing energy, water, waste/material and financial helpers under the approved common contracts. Its dependency manifest declares available analytical capabilities and their source skill files, not production activation or proof of independent reliability. Core calculations remain vendor/jurisdiction neutral. No domain methodology, emission factor, tariff, engineering design or financial preference is invented by composition.

`python -m scripts.run_operations request.json` accepts the common input envelope with skill=sustainable-operations. `run_operations(state,parameters)` requires exactly steps, opportunities, planning_review and result_id. It returns the common result plus one batch proposal at the original base revision and candidate revision +1. Each intermediate analytical result and domain reference is retained in the candidate state; intermediate revisions are execution context, not separately persisted changes. Existing state is unchanged. Exit 0 includes blocked/partial analytical results; exit 2 is an invalid request. No state file or external system is written.

steps is nonempty and ordered, each {skill,parameters}. Skill must be in the bounded existing energy/water/resource/finance runner allowlist. Each helper enforces its own exact inputs and source/period/unit/review rules. Every step and the composition use new distinct result IDs. Arbitrary code, scripts, recursive operations composition and external actions are unsupported. Blocked source calculations retain their gaps; other independent steps can still produce supported evidence. A proposed registration does not manufacture a saving from a baseline or incomplete calculation.

planning_review requires confirmed=true, objective, scope_boundary, rationale, known evidence_ids, boolean coverage_complete and planning_period={start,end}. Planning dates remain distinct from source reporting periods and financial study horizons. Coverage is supplied judgment rather than independent authentication.

opportunities is a nonempty register. Each candidate has exactly id, domain=energy/water/waste/materials, description, owner (role or null), known evidence_ids, assessment_result_ids, business_case_result_id (or null), business_case_applicability (or null), actions and interacts_with. IDs are new and unique; previous opportunity entities are preserved. Every assessment link must be freshly executed by a step, and at least one must belong to the declared physical domain's checked result list or a blocked analytical step in that domain. Source results and their measurement bases remain visible; baseline amounts are not labeled savings, reused source results are not additive benefits, and registration supplies no extra numerical result.

An optional interpretation_result_ids list links known distinct common results from the declared physical domain's interpretation skills. These may be earlier recorded assessments rather than newly executed numerical steps; each must retain source evidence. The separate interpretation_dependencies manifest section identifies supported skill files and remains development pending review. Wrong-domain, unsourced, unknown or duplicate links block candidate registration. Linked interpretation evidence is included in candidate/result provenance; original diagnostics, assumptions, gaps and reviews stay in result history. Partial or blocked interpretations qualify candidate coverage. Common-contract validation establishes references and preservation, not source authenticity, current operational applicability or a rerun of reasoning. Fresh physical assessments remain mandatory and prior interpretation is not relabeled a reproduced calculation.

A nonnull business_case_result_id links an evidence-linked composed business case, not its annual cash-flow variant. The case is rerun and its metrics checked against current source inputs. business_case_applicability requires confirmed=true, known evidence_ids, rationale, scope_boundary and project_ids naming actual alternatives in that case. This is supplied applicability review, not automatic proof that a financial or physical effect belongs to an opportunity. Both link/applicability are null when no case exists. Financial and physical periods/definitions remain in that case rather than being flattened into a score. A missing case adds an economic-assessment data need rather than an invented return. Missing opportunity ownership, incomplete linked assessments or declared coverage also produce qualified candidate records and data needs. No portfolio total is calculated.

Each candidate has at least one action {description,owner,kind=data_collection/implementation,target_date,prerequisite_review_ids}. Dates lie within the explicit planning interval, owners/descriptions are supplied, and prerequisite IDs resolve to the retained review ledger. Every action stays proposed and implementation_authorized=false. Implementation proposals automatically include a new open ENGINEERING_REVIEW_REQUIRED gate for operational applicability, service/quality, interactions and design. Data gathering proposals retain supplied prerequisites without being mislabeled approved implementation. Opportunity-level unassigned ownership remains a gap even when a proposed action has an owner.

Optional dependency planning adds id and depends_on together to every selected action. IDs are unique within the plan; prerequisites reference other selected action IDs, may cross domains, and cannot repeat, reference themselves, form a cycle or have a target date later than the dependent action. Legacy date-only requests remain supported unchanged. Invalid graphs add no candidates or new review gates; checked source analyses remain in the proposal. OPERATIONS_ACTION_SEQUENCE emits a deterministic topological order with target-date/ID tie ordering, completion_verified=false and implementation_authorized=false. This is declared precedence, not a financial ranking, proof of completion, resource-capacity schedule or critical-path calculation. Same-day precedence is permitted; no duration is inferred. Review prerequisites and physical candidate interactions remain separate from action dependencies.

Interactions identify other selected candidates and must be declared symmetrically. Source compatibility, opportunity mechanisms, applicability, ownership and hidden interactions require substantive review; IDs and field presence cannot authenticate them. Invalid registration yields a blocked result without adding candidate entities; valid source calculations already executed remain in the batch proposal. All prior gaps, assumptions and review obligations survive. Open reviews are never resolved by registration.

The result records OPERATIONS_PLAN with candidate entities, proposed actions, source execution trace, planning scope, null portfolio_total and implementation_authorized=false. Entities enter state.opportunities as {id,evidence_ids,attributes}, with status=candidate and links to source assessments/business cases. OPERATIONS_INPUTS records the reproducible request. The result need not emit fabricated count or saving metrics: numerical outputs remain in their source domain results with their original periods, units, evidence and uncertainty.

This initial composition supports deterministic analytical execution and candidate/action registration. Independently evaluated cross-domain interpretation, resource-aware scheduling, ongoing performance tracking and organization-wide end-to-end evidence remain further work. The user approved the initial composition and dependency manifest for continued development on 2026-10-05; see operations-review.md. Router activation, specialist release readiness and public v1 are later gates.
