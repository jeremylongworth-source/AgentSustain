# Inventory evidence for original claims

`assess-inventory-claim-evidence` extends the bound-material claims pipeline with a separate `claim-inventory-0.1.0` source bridge and `claim-evidence-0.3.0` assessment report. It is draft infrastructure following reviewed snapshot d99a70b; the subsequent material and inventory interfaces require scoped owner review. Existing claim operations and their saved outputs retain their behavior.

The request contains the eight bound-material parameters plus `inventory_review`. Run `python -m scripts.run_claims request.json` with the common envelope. The result retains original material, evidence, factors, earlier results, assumptions, gaps and outstanding reviews in an append-only proposal. It emits no metric, stronger marketing text, legal finding or publication authority.

## Explicit inventory context

`inventory_review` has exact fields `inventory_result_id`, `boundary_id`, `period`, `as_of_date`, `subject_id`, `coverage`, `facility_ids`, `coverage_evidence_ids`, `evidence_fit`, `scope_2_method`, `gwp_basis`, `rationale`, `gas_coverage_assessment`, `reviewer_role`. Coverage is `selected_sources`, `whole_subject` or `unknown`; evidence fitness is `reviewed_supporting`, `unverified` or `irrelevant`; the scope 2 method is `location`, `market` or `unknown`. These are attributed observations, not authenticated professional judgments.

The selected inventory must resolve to a supported organization inventory with one metric. The bridge reproduces each selected scope/category account, each accepted CO2e leaf using its recorded activity/factor/policy, and the inventory selection. Full metrics include value, units, period, boundary, method, uncertainty and calculation lineage. Method reports and evidence references must match. Verification IDs alone are normalized; source records remain unchanged. Claims do not replay unsupported arithmetic from memory.

Accepted leaves, current factor/evidence snapshots and full source reports are retained. Unaccepted components remain explicitly recorded without fabricated quantities; a supported partial subtotal can retain these gaps. Missing or changed factors yield `EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED`. A broken metric/report/uncertainty/lineage or falsely completed source with new replay failures blocks with `CLAIM_INVENTORY_REQUIRED`. Recomputed status may reflect later inherited gaps; those gaps remain in the claim proposal and cannot be resolved by replay.

Source fitness requires a matching organization, boundary, period, declared GWP basis and selected scope 2 method, current versioned evidence accessed by the review date, and supporting coverage evidence for the reporting period. Inventory and claim review dates must match. Synthetic sources cannot enter real-use mode. The existing inventory engine is organization-based; facility and product claims have no attribution bridge here.

## Declared coverage and claims states

Whole-subject quantity and inventory criteria need reproduced declared coverage: the original inventory, accounts and accepted leaves are completed without stored gaps; scope 1 and one scope 2 account are present; all fifteen scope 3 categories are explicitly applicable or not applicable; the selected method and GWP basis match; reviewed facility IDs equal the organizational boundary; scope 1/2 source registers cover those facilities; and the boundary has no exclusions. An applicable category without a supported result cannot pass replay. Unknown/excluded categories, incomplete sources, gaps, missing facilities or missing scope accounts withhold the candidate. These conservative conditions do not cover organizations whose valid boundary needs explicit exclusions, missing/no-source scope declarations or additional facility attribution methods; those cases remain unresolved.

`declared_scope_coverage_reproduced` describes reproduction of this supplied scope/source/category record. It never verifies external completeness, source authenticity, organization-wide source/gas discovery or historical source provenance. The supplied gas-coverage assessment remains a professional judgment. Alternative scope 2 methods are never added together. Current evidence snapshots are preserved; older core calculations did not archive source bytes, so replay cannot authenticate those original bytes.

The original claim text and literal numerical interpretation still require exact material binding. A whole-subject claim additionally requires an inventory criterion; omission remains insufficient. A partial selected subtotal can support a qualified selected-source statement without becoming a whole inventory. An otherwise suitable conflicting numerical interpretation produces potentially misleading, without a legal finding. Inventory arithmetic supplies none of the distinct reduction, credit or residual-emission methods: those criteria remain insufficient even when documentary observations are favourable. Carbon-neutral and net-zero claims therefore cannot become substantiated through this bridge alone. Jurisdiction and aggregate professional review remain open; unconditional aggregate support and publication authority are unavailable.

`CLAIM_INVENTORY_SOURCE_CHECK` retains the full source bridge, current evidence/factors, unsupported components and explicit false flags for external completeness, authenticity, net-emission/neutrality determination and publication. `CLAIM_INVENTORY_INPUTS` retains the exact request parameters. Existing bound-material and criterion reports remain available for inspection.

The [fictional workflow evidence](../evaluations/sus20-inventory-claim.md) and adversarial checks demonstrate these boundaries. Actual organization/source completeness, broader gases and scientific methods, reduction/credit/residual substantiation, current jurisdiction/source/rights review, independent end-to-end organization evaluation, individual skills, routing/skillsets and release readiness remain open. This increment does not close SUS-20 or the full roadmap goal.
