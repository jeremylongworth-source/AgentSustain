# Jurisdiction-module contract

Status: initial SUS-18 executable foundation with fictional tests; scoped contract review, real source packs and independent organization evaluation remain open. Canada is the reference pack for SUS-19; no actual regulatory rules are currently implemented.

Canonical location is `standards/jurisdictions/`. Country modules may contain federal and subnational rules. Canadian subject groups are applicability, carbon, climate-disclosure, environmental-claims, energy, waste, procurement and reporting.

Every rule needs jurisdiction ID, official authority/source and clause locator, version, effective-from/to interval, retrieval date, legal status, subject, required company attributes, screening logic, exceptions, uncertainty, and reviewer requirement. Null effective-to means open ended, not permanently current. Proposed rules cannot be treated as law. Recheck official sources at use time when currency matters; preserve the version used in historical results.

Inputs include operating locations, legal entity attributes, size/sector thresholds where relevant and reporting period. Outputs are preliminary applicable/potentially_applicable/not_applicable/undetermined screens, evidence references, missing attributes, basis, uncertainty and review requirements. Insufficient company facts, missing version or conflicting federal/subnational interpretation yields undetermined plus gaps; definitive legal conclusions require legal review.

Acceptance scenarios cover threshold boundaries, missing facts, overlapping jurisdictions, effective dates, amendments and repealed rules. Jurisdiction packs cannot alter neutral calculation schemas or invent factors. Specific Canadian requirements must be researched from official sources during SUS-19 rather than inferred from this design.

## Initial executable screening contract

`python -m scripts.run_jurisdiction REQUEST.json` accepts the existing common input envelope with `skill: screen-jurisdiction-applicability`. This is an initial helper/CLI foundation, not an installed skill, active jurisdiction registration, Canadian rule pack or router implementation. Common state schemas remain unchanged. Successful evaluation returns a partial advisory result, no metrics and an append-only proposal with an open `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` obligation. Existing evidence, quantities, boundaries, periods, results, factors, gaps, assumptions and review decisions survive. Invalid screening data returns a checked blocked result; an invalid envelope or duplicate result ID returns CLI exit 2. The CLI never writes its request or commits proposed state.

Exact parameters are `pack_pins`, `facts`, `rule_reviews`, `screening_review`, `fixture_mode`, `result_id`. Fixture mode is a boolean; synthetic packs require true. `screening_review` contains exactly `boundary_id`, `period`, `as_of_date`, `scope`, `evidence_ids`, `evidence_fit`, `rationale`, `reviewer_role`. Boundary and reporting period must match current state. Source fitness is reviewed_supporting, unverified or irrelevant; the review date cannot precede the reporting period. A fitness label is a caller's evidence judgment, not independent legal verification.

### Pinned modules

Each pin contains exactly `path` (relative to standards/jurisdictions) and exact-byte `sha256`. External paths, traversal, changed bytes and two selected versions of the same pack ID block. Scoped Git attributes enforce LF JSON bytes. Pack contents have exactly `id`, `version`, `synthetic`, `jurisdiction_attribute`, `attributes`, `rules`, `scope`, `limitations`. Nonfictional sources require HTTPS primary-source locators; a URL alone does not authenticate or approve the source. Pack/version selection remains explicit, with no automatic latest version, amendment precedence or historical state migration.

Each attribute has `id`, `type`, `unit`, `definition`. Types are number, string, boolean and string_set. Conflicting definitions across packs block. The jurisdiction attribute must be a string_set. Company jurisdiction membership is evaluated from an explicit evidenced roster; the helper does not infer country membership from a province code or use the state's jurisdiction list as proof of legal scope.

Every rule has `id`, `jurisdiction`, `subject`, `source`, `legal_status`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, `condition`, `exceptions`, `exceptions_complete`, `uncertainty`, `reviewer_requirement`. Source has `authority`, `locator`, `clause`, `version` (substantive or null), `retrieved`. Status is in_force, proposed, repealed or unknown. Dates are ISO days with inclusive endpoints. An unknown effective_from stays unknown. Null effective_to is open ended; a repealed rule requires its last effective day. Reversed/malformed dates and source retrieval after screening block. These fields are curated source records, not automatically verified statements of law.

Predicates are bounded declarative objects, never executable code. A leaf has exactly `attribute`, `operator`, `value`; operators are numeric eq/ne/gt/gte/lt/lte, string eq/ne/in, boolean eq/ne, and string_set intersects. Nonempty all/any groups combine leaves with three-valued true/false/unknown logic. Expressions are limited to depth 12 and 128 nodes. Each exception has `id`, `description`, `condition`. Incomplete exception coverage remains explicit and can prevent a positive screen; an evidenced true exception can exclude the rule in the preliminary screen without authorizing an exemption. Overlapping rules are retained individually without conflict resolution or precedence inference.

### Company facts and current-source reviews

Each fact has exactly `attribute_id`, `value`, `unit`, `boundary_id`, `period`, `evidence_ids`, `evidence_fit`, `rationale`, `uncertainty`, `coverage_complete`. Numeric values are explicit `{lower, upper}` ranges with finite bounds or null unknown bounds; booleans are not numbers. Other values match the defined type or are null. Reversed ranges, unit mismatches, wrong company boundary, duplicate/undefined facts and invalid references block. Unit conversions, legal-entity aggregation and unstated counts are not invented.

A supported fact requires nonempty supporting evidence, complete declared coverage and a fact period covering the whole reporting period. Every evidence record must match boundary/unit, cover the fact period, carry a source version and not have future access relative to the review. A partial-period or unsuitable record remains an unverified fact. A range crossing a threshold produces unknown; no endpoint is selected. Missing values and incomplete jurisdiction rosters cannot become zero or absence. Qualitative source uncertainty remains in the complete record; numerical uncertainty must be supplied through the declared range. Source reading and the caller's fitness decision remain necessary to determine whether those bounds are defensible.

Each rule review has exactly `pack_id`, `rule_id`, `checked_as_of`, `evidence_ids`, `evidence_fit`, `rationale`. It must select an actual pinned rule, with at least one evidence source matching its exact locator/version and no future accessed record. checked_as_of must equal the screening date for supported currency, and cannot postdate it. Missing, stale, unfit or unrelated source reviews leave applicability undetermined. Full law/exceptions coverage and actual currency still require qualified primary-source review; a supplied date does not certify them.

### Temporal and authority boundaries

A reporting period is split at the selected rule's effective boundaries. Every segment retains its period, recorded effective-interval relationship and preliminary status. A rule active for only part of the reporting year stays potentially_applicable overall. Proposed rules cannot become in-force obligations; unknown dates/versions/status remain unresolved. Repealed rules can still be screened for their supplied historical active interval. Conditions proven false can produce a preliminary not_applicable screen only under supported source/review context; this is not legal clearance or an approved exemption.

`JURISDICTION_SCREEN` retains complete packs/pins, company facts/source snapshots, rule/source-review snapshots, every predicate trace, exception verdict, temporal segment and unused fact ID. Legal applicability, compliance, exemption authority, obligation adoption, publication and whole-organization coverage remain unverified. These initial semantics extend the approved interface and require scoped architecture/development review; test success cannot substitute for owner or legal review. Real Canadian sources, independent raw-source interpretation, amendments/version comparison, broader temporal company facts and organization coverage remain subsequent work.

[The fictional foundation capture](../evaluations/sus18-jurisdiction-foundation.md) records author-led examples and actual CLI replay. No actual Canadian threshold, source, emission factor, legal obligation or exemption is encoded by these fixtures.
