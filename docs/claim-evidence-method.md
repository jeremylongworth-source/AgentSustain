# Initial jurisdiction-neutral claim evidence review

The draft `claim-evidence-0.1.0` helper in `scripts/claims_review.py` preserves an original claim dossier, records supplied criterion observations and checks scoped source evidence. It reproduces selected gas-chain quantities and blocks use beyond their supported scope. It does not automatically read/classify arbitrary text/images, authenticate reviewer judgments, determine legal adequacy or authorize publishing. Shared schemas and existing calculation/jurisdiction methods remain unchanged. This material interface awaits scoped review after approved foundation 898d790.

## Input and original context

Use `python -m scripts.run_claims request.json` with operation `assess-claim-evidence`. Parameters are exactly `claim`, `classification_review`, `criteria`, `claim_review`, `fixture_mode`, `result_id`. The result ID must be unused; fixture mode is explicitly boolean. No individual skill is generated.

Claim fields are `id`, `text`, `kind`, `subject_kind`, `subject_id`, `scope`, `period`, `boundary_id`, `material_evidence_ids`, `representation_dates`, `channel`, `audience`, `purpose`, `qualifications`. Original text and qualifications are returned unchanged. Representation dates are distinct nonfuture ISO dates, separate from the evidence/reporting period. Material sources contain those dates, have current versions/nonfuture access and match the boundary. Missing dates/material/scope remain unverified rather than inferred. This initial evidence period matches the state reporting period; broader target/lifecycle/representation temporal models remain open.

Kinds are quantified, factual_content, comparative, carbon_neutral, net_zero, future_goal, general_benefit, mixed or unknown. Subject is organization, facility or product, with known organization/facility IDs checked against current state. Product identity is a declared dossier identity; this helper has no authenticated product/source bridge. Scope is whole_subject, selected_sources or unknown. No scope/type is guessed from a word such as green or neutral.

Classification review has `kind`, `evidence_ids`, `evidence_fit`, `rationale`, `reviewer_role`, bound to original material IDs and the declared kind. Claim review has `boundary_id`, `period`, `as_of_date`, `evidence_ids`, `evidence_fit`, `scope`, `rationale`, `reviewer_role`. Supporting fitness and source versions/access are required. These are attributed caller observations, not authenticated independent reading or qualified legal approval. Known material metadata does not prove that the supplied textual/quantity interpretation accurately reflects the full original artifact.

## Criteria and evidence checks

Each claim requires evidence, boundary, period, method, qualifications, general_impression and jurisdiction criteria. Quantified/content claims additionally require quantity; comparisons require baseline/comparability; carbon-neutral/net-zero claims require inventory/reductions/credits/residuals; future goals require plan/progress. Omitted criteria stay explicit unknowns. Unknown/mixed classification remains insufficient until assessed.

Each criterion has `id`, `assertion`, `boundary_id`, `period`, `unit`, `evidence_ids`, `source_result_ids`, `evidence_fit`, `verdict`, `quantity`, `qualifications`, `qualification_visible`, `rationale`. Verdict is supports, qualified, unknown or contradicts; this is a supplied inspection judgment, not an automatic document or scientific/legal interpretation. Source/unit/period/boundary/version/current-access checks can withhold it. A qualified observation needs original qualification strings and supplied visibility, never newly strengthened marketing text. Documentary evidence covers the evidence period; source-result observations bind intersecting source evidence and matching metric units.

Quantity is null or exactly `{metric_id, expected_value}`. Quantity criteria require a reproduced source result and a selected metric. The supplied numerical interpretation is checked against that metric using Decimal values. A known mismatch with otherwise suitable context produces a potentially-misleading candidate even if the supplied verdict says supports. An unknown expected value or missing source never becomes zero. This is a consistency check of the provided interpretation; it does not extract the asserted value from unstructured claim text or a screenshot.

Supported source replays are calculate-gas-mass, convert-gas-mass-to-co2e and build-facility-gas-ledger. Complete metric/value/unit/period/boundary/method/calculation/uncertainty, source report and evidence IDs must reproduce against current state. Source assessment date must match the claim review; current claim review does not silently refresh source currency. Altered numeric/report/source/uncertainty/lineage records block the review. Unsupported source operations remain unverified; they are not reconstructed from memory. A selected blocked factor/GWP result propagates EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED and cannot support a quantity.

A ledger can support only a matching facility's selected-source interpretation, with its entire declared roster reproduced. An incomplete ledger subtotal cannot support an unspecified selected total. Raw gas/conversion records bind organization activity only; they cannot supply product/facility attribution. Source-fragment claims, non-GHG numeric methods, original-test protocols and other domain bridges remain open. Whole-subject inventory substantiation is deliberately unavailable through source subtotals; neutrality claims retain missing inventory/credit/reduction/residual requirements.

## States and authority

Criterion states use the roadmap's SUPPORTED, SUPPORTED_WITH_QUALIFICATION, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, POTENTIALLY_MISLEADING and PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED labels. Supported/qualified criterion states describe scoped attributed evidence candidates, not legal approval or verified source authenticity. The jurisdiction criterion stays professionally unresolved because core review determines no jurisdiction applicability.

Aggregate state is potentially misleading if a suitable criterion conflicts, insufficient if an applicable criterion is unknown, otherwise professional review required. This initial operation never emits unconditional aggregate support or publication approval. Later independent/qualified review and controlled approval workflows must address the full material and all applicable requirements; passing arithmetic cannot substitute for them.

`CLAIM_EVIDENCE_REVIEW` retains unchanged claim/material/context, classification/assessment records, all criterion/source/result snapshots, quantities and unknowns. `CLAIM_REVIEW_INPUTS` retains exact parameters. Legal applicability, source authenticity, authenticated professional judgments, publication authorization and marketing-text-change flags stay false. Each output creates open professional claim and legal reviews and preserves all inherited factor/engineering/legal/source gaps and results. It creates no metric, stronger statement, filing or external action.
