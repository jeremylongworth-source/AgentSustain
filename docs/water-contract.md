# SUS-11 water execution

Apply common state/result contracts and the data execution contract. Reviewed 2026-10-04: [GRI 303: Water and Effluents 2018](https://www.globalreporting.org/publications/documents/english/gri-303-water-and-effluents-2018) distinguishes withdrawal, discharge and consumption and emphasizes local context and storage changes. This is a methodological reference, not a core reporting adapter, legal applicability determination or conformance claim. Do not equate purchased/withdrawn volume with consumption, combine reuse with new withdrawals, or infer basin stress from national averages.

## Runner

`python -m scripts.run_water request.json` uses the common request; `run_water(state, skill, parameters)` returns a result and checked proposal. Exit 0 includes blocked results; exit 2 is invalid input. Supported results append to state.water; no state file is written. Nonnegative finite volumes must convert through supported L/m3 units and match current reporting period/boundary. Unknown quantity is not zero; mass needs its own documented transformation rather than an assumed density.

build-water-baseline accepts quantities, coverage_review and result_id. Each quantity has exactly metric_id, basis, facility_id, source_kind, catchment (name or null) and evidence_ids supporting its context. Every basis must match the reviewed basis: withdrawal, discharge, consumption, internal_reuse or metered_use. facility_id must belong to the reporting boundary. source_kind describes the actual source/measurement context; an internal_reuse source cannot be counted as withdrawal. Inspect source records rather than relying on label spelling to discover hidden reuse or upstream/downstream overlap.

coverage_review contains confirmed=true, basis, measurement_boundary, nonoverlap_assessment, coverage, rationale, boolean coverage_complete and evidence_ids. Optional coverage_details maps each selected metric to distinct evidence_id/source_fragment records under the data-baseline contract. Total/components, duplicated meters and transfer/reuse flows cannot be counted twice. The helper rejects duplicate quantities, aggregate/component lineage overlap and unreconciled shared documents; hidden coverage overlaps remain source-review work. It converts selected same-basis quantities to m3. Incomplete coverage or unknown catchment creates a gap while retaining supported volume. Review/context fields are supplied assessments, not authenticated basin/facility facts.

calculate-water-intensity accepts baseline_result_id, denominator_id, denominator_review and result_id. Reproduce the baseline from current source quantities/context before division. Denominator review requires confirmed=true, exact metric_id, definition, unit, rationale and evidence_ids. Use a positive same-period/boundary activity; unsupported/unknown units or zero denominator block. The name preserves whether the numerator is withdrawal, consumption or another basis. No occupancy/product-mix, currency or production inference is performed.

identify-water-hotspots accepts baseline_result_id and result_id. Revalidate and rank mutually exclusive selected volume contributions, with shares of the selected baseline where positive. Zero baseline gives zero volumes without shares. Retain facility/source/catchment context in the baseline record. Volume contribution is not automatically local impact, stress, quality or inefficiency.

## Dependency and opportunities

assess-water-dependency and identify-water-efficiency-opportunities are instruction workflows. Dependency needs supported process/service requirements, quantity/quality and seasonality, source/utility reliability, location/catchment, alternatives and downstream/community considerations. A basin tool observation must retain source/version, geographic resolution and applicable date; it is screening evidence, not a facility-specific prediction or permit/legal conclusion. Missing local data yields a gap instead of a guessed stress score. Advanced climate-risk integration follows SUS-16.

Opportunities need an observed mechanism such as documented leakage, schedule or process loss and explicit service/quality constraints. Reuse may reduce external withdrawal while increasing internal circulation; do not add both as savings or infer lower consumption/impact. Record owners, interactions, model assumptions, measurement needs and technical review. No default savings percentage, safe water-quality/treatment instruction, verified savings or investment approval is supplied. Derived consumption balances, storage adjustments and advanced process models remain future supported methods; existing consumption metrics must already have defensible provenance.
