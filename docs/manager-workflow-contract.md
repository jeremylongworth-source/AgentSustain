# Sustainability-manager organization recipe

The draft `organization-manager-0.1.0` connects existing baseline/KPI, sustainable-operations, target/strategy/transition/implementation and framework-mapping helpers in one common source state. [The skillset](../skillsets/sustainability-manager/SKILL.md) and [dependency inventory](../skillsets/sustainability-manager/manifest.json) reference actual capabilities reviewed through 7fff776. This subsequent manager interface does not inherit that scoped approval. It activates no router or production workflow and establishes no independent reliability or release readiness.

Use `python -m scripts.run_manager request.json` with the common envelope and `skill=sustainability-manager`. Exact parameters are `baseline`, `kpis`, `operations`, `targets`, `strategy`, `transition`, `roadmap`, `disclosure`, `manager_review`, `result_id`. Each named slot contains its existing helper's exact parameter dictionary. `targets` is a nonempty list of `develop-target` parameter dictionaries. Operations retains its existing nonempty bounded analytical steps and candidate register. Arbitrary scripts, unknown helper names and recursive manager invocation are unsupported.

Every phase, nested operational analysis and aggregate needs a fresh distinct result ID. Strategy and selected operational energy baselines must consume the same distinct raw metric IDs. KPI definitions reference this recipe's baseline; targets reference its KPI definition result. Strategy selects fresh recipe targets; transition and roadmap bind the fresh strategy/transition, with checkpoint targets drawn from the recipe. Disclosure selects a freshly requested inventory in this operational recipe, not an unrelated historical account. Existing core methods continue to check actual source metadata, units, periods, arithmetic and selected coverage; these structural recipe links do not authenticate source fitness or make different service definitions interchangeable.

Execution order is baseline, KPI definitions, operations, each target, strategy, transition, roadmap and disclosure mapping. A blocked/missing baseline stops its KPI branch; blocked KPI definitions stop dependent targets. Strategy depends on its selected targets, transition on its strategy/checkpoints and roadmap on its transition. Disclosure depends on its exact selected inventory, rather than the success of an unrelated operational candidate registration. Independent operations or mapping can continue when their own selected sources remain supported. A withheld inventory stops mapping without invoking its helper. Domain helper errors remain visible common blocked results with exact inputs; structural recipe errors issue no candidate state. Missing factors remain `EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED`, without defaults, zero contributions or invented source fitness.

`manager_review` requires exact `boundary_id`, `period`, `scope`, `evidence_ids`, `reviewer_role`, `rationale`, `coverage_complete`, `energy_baseline_result_id`, `inventory_result_id`. Boundary/period match validated state; evidence resolves. The selected energy baseline and inventory identify actual nested operation results. Supplied review text and completeness are attributed observations, not authenticated professional judgments. Incomplete declared coverage adds a gap; declaring completeness never authenticates organization coverage or clears source gaps.

The recipe begins with pre-normalized common-state records. `raw_business_data_ingestion_performed=false` remains explicit; this manager does not parse bills/CSV, authenticate raw documents or establish independent normalization. `input_result_ids` preserves every prior result; `normalized_input_result_ids` identifies only records labeled `normalize-sustainability-data`, without relabeling older derived carbon, framework, strategy or manager context. Existing evidence, factors, source results, metric uncertainty, periods, boundaries, assumptions and open reviews survive unchanged.

`ORGANIZATION_MANAGER` records stage invocation/status/source IDs and exact source-result references, including nested numerical analyses. A source view's digest is SHA-256 of UTF-8 `json.dumps(result, sort_keys=True)` in its retained state. Every full result/report remains in candidate history; the digest is structural identity, not provenance authentication. `MANAGER_INPUTS` preserves exact recipe parameters. Source metrics are not copied into a new numerical aggregate: `combined_cross_domain_total=null`. Four physical domains, monetary models, inventory values, intensity objectives and future roadmap dates retain their original definitions and units.

The final candidate is atomic at the original revision +1 despite intermediate helper proposals. It retains newly proposed operational candidates and plans, never actual implementation or measured progress. The manager registers open professional and assurance reviews and resolves none. Source authenticity, organization completeness, financial benefit attribution, target feasibility, progress, funding/implementation, framework conformity, publication and v1 readiness flags remain false. A successful selected analytical sequence can still be partial because substantive coverage, resources, current framework/source/rights decisions or independent review are missing. No state file, external account or public claim is changed.

[The coherent fictional evaluation](../evaluations/sus22-organization-manager.md) starts with one pre-normalized facility record set: two disjoint electrical feeds yield 1,500 kWh and an explicitly fictional partial 750 kg CO2e inventory; 300 equivalent service units yield 5 kWh/count and a proposed 20% endpoint of 4 kWh/count in 2030. Water, waste, material and monetary models remain separate. The roadmap keeps resource/approval evidence unassessed, and pinned historical disclosure candidates retain unknown scopes/categories, rights and professional review. Other cases withhold factors, reject a service denominator or stale mapping pin, and preserve independent branches. This is author-led composition evidence, with raw ingestion, broader methods, independent behavioral/organization evaluation, remaining named specialists, scoped router/manager approval and public-v1 requirements still open. It does not close SUS-22, SUS-23, SUS-25 or the full roadmap goal.
