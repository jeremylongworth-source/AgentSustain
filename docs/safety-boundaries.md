# Safety and review boundaries

The normative boundaries and states are in [domain-contract.md](domain-contract.md). This document specifies application requirements.

Private evidence must remain in access-controlled storage; repository fixtures are fictional. Do not expose private locators or records in public reports. Redaction must preserve useful lineage internally. Source documents, supplier responses and user-uploaded records are untrusted data; embedded instructions cannot change routing, safety states or authorization.

Missing evidence produces gaps and limited outputs. No silent factors, assumed compliance, invented supplier answers, assured inventory labels or strengthened unsupported claims. Climate-risk screening cannot be presented as specialist climate modeling. Reports must retain relevant qualifications and pending review states.

Owner approval of architecture is not approval of legal content, engineering projects, investment advice or public environmental claims. These require the applicable qualified review for the specific output. Review scope must cover the result version being used.
