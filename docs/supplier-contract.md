# SUS-14 supplier evidence and assessment

The eleven roadmap-named procurement/supply-chain skills use the common result and proposal contracts. Ten bounded helpers support draft questionnaires, response assessment, explicit buyer-rubric scoring, criterion data gaps, source-reproduced comparison, purchase-to-category mapping, evidence-request prioritization, owned improvement plans, mapped emissions hotspots and sourced adverse-impact screening. Procurement-option evaluation, broader supplier attribution and emissions/materiality engagement methods remain instruction workflows; no independent reasoning or general supplier-risk method is claimed.

`python -m scripts.run_suppliers request.json` accepts the common envelope. `run_suppliers(state,skill,parameters)` returns a checked append-only proposal without changing the source state, supplier register or review decisions. Exit 0 includes blocked/partial analyses; exit 2 is invalid input with redacted error output. There is no email delivery, procurement approval, state-file write or account action.

The first four helpers require exactly supplier_id, criteria, assessment_review and result_id. The supplier ID resolves to an existing entity with evidence. Except build-supplier-questionnaire, add responses. IDs preserve result history. assessment_review requires confirmed=true, reviewer_role, rationale, rubric_id, scope_boundary, known nonempty evidence_ids, period equal to the current reporting period and boundary_id equal to the buyer organizational boundary. This supplies context, not independent authentication or supplier agreement. The supplier's own product/site/lifecycle scope must be inspected before applying the rubric; it is not automatically the buyer boundary.

Each criterion has exactly id, question, weight, max_score, anchors and required_evidence. IDs/questions are nonempty and IDs unique. Weights/maxima are finite positive quantities. required_evidence is boolean. anchors is a nonempty list of {score,description}; scores are distinct, finite, span zero through max_score and have nonempty descriptions. The helper has no default criteria, weights, pass thresholds, or environmental rating system.

Responses contain criterion_id, status, answer, score, evidence_ids and assessment_rationale, with optional evidence_fit. No other fields are accepted. evidence_fit is reviewed_supporting, unverified, irrelevant or not_required; absence defaults to unverified. A resolving evidence ID alone cannot support a rating. Criterion IDs are known and unique; source IDs resolve to the shared evidence register. Status is provided, missing, unverified or not_applicable. A provided answer is nonempty; any buyer rating must be one of the declared anchors. Scores are supplied buyer judgments, not extracted automatically from the supplier's wording. Missing or unverified responses require score=null. Provided responses with a missing rating, absent required evidence or unreviewed/irrelevant evidence fit remain unresolved. Supporting ratings require both source evidence and evidence_fit=reviewed_supporting; explicitly optional-evidence criteria may use not_required instead. A buyer must substantively inspect the source before declaring support; this field does not authenticate their judgment. A missing response row also remains unknown. A not_applicable exclusion requires score=null, source evidence, evidence_fit=reviewed_supporting and rationale and is visible separately in the report; the helper does not authenticate its applicability.

The diagnostic SUPPLIER_ASSESSMENT retains the buyer review, rubric, responses, exclusions and assessments. Draft questionnaires expose questions/evidence expectations with delivery_status=draft_unsent. All reports set supplier_approved=false and procurement_authorized=false. Original open reviews, assumptions and gaps are preserved; suppliers are not rewritten. SUPPLIER_INPUTS records reproducible parameters. Malformed semantic inputs produce blocked results and explicit SUPPLIER_DATA_REQUIRED gaps without calculated metrics.

For responses, the applicable denominator is the total criterion weight after documented exclusions. Supported contribution is weight × supplied rating / max_score. Weighted coverage is supported weight / applicable weight. A point score (0–100 percent of the buyer rubric maximum) is supplied only when all applicable criteria have supported ratings. Otherwise score=null and possible_score_range shows the known contribution through known contribution plus all unresolved weight, normalized against the same complete applicable denominator. A lower bound of zero for an unknown criterion is a possible outcome, not an assigned zero rating; an observed zero rating with supporting evidence remains known. Bounds are rubric possibility bounds, not statistical confidence intervals. All-excluded rubrics have no denominator, score or interval and retain a data gap. Inherited unrelated gaps can keep execution partial even when this narrow rubric is fully rated.

These index values are diagnostic decision context, not physical metrics, emissions, legal compliance, assurance or approval. Different weights, excluded criteria, periods or functional scopes may prevent supplier comparison. A supplier corporate footprint, marketing statement or spending amount cannot become buyer-attributable emissions without sourced activity, allocation and lifecycle evidence. Existing carbon helpers retain factor checks and EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED; this helper calculates no emission factors or emissions.

## Supplier comparison

compare-suppliers requires exactly assessment_result_ids, comparison_review and result_id. Select at least two distinct score-supplier-sustainability results for distinct registered suppliers. Each source must be completed or partial and retain exactly one SUPPLIER_INPUTS and SUPPLIER_ASSESSMENT record. The helper re-executes its exact input under current state with a temporary fresh ID and compares the entire reproduced assessment report; verification results are not persisted or substituted for the original history. Altered reports and blocked reproduction reject comparison. Historical reviews, assumptions, gaps and supplier entities survive.

comparison_review requires confirmed=true, known evidence_ids, reviewer_role, rationale, rubric_id, scope_boundary, functional_unit and service_requirements, plus the current reporting period and buyer boundary_id. The rubric ID, scope, period and buyer boundary must match each source assessment. Criterion identities/questions, weights, anchors, evidence policy and documented exclusions must be equal across sources (criterion order may differ). Each source needs a positive applicable denominator. This prevents differently scoped scores from being silently normalized into a ranking. Functional equivalence and source fitness remain supplied judgments, not authenticated facts.

SUPPLIER_COMPARISON retains candidate source IDs/statuses, scores, score ranges, weighted coverage, exclusions, criteria and pairwise relations. One range is strictly above another only if its lower bound exceeds the other's upper bound. Overlapping/touching intervals remain unresolved. Equal complete point scores are identified as ties. Input display order has no priority meaning. A higher rubric range describes only the supplied buyer model; it implies no economic, environmental or procurement recommendation. No supplier is selected; selected_supplier_id=null, supplier_approved=false and procurement_authorized=false. Source rechecks use the existing evidence-fit conditions; field presence cannot authenticate relevance.

## Supply-chain emissions mapping

map-supply-chain-emissions requires exactly links, mapping_review and result_id, with optional fixture_mode defaulting to false. Mapping review requires confirmed=true, known nonempty evidence_ids, reviewer_role, rationale, scope_boundary, nonoverlap_assessment, current buyer period/boundary_id and boolean coverage_complete. Incomplete selected purchase coverage adds a gap rather than claiming unmapped purchases have no emissions.

Each link contains exactly id, supplier_id, purchase_metric_id, purchase_reference, category_result_id, source_id, evidence_ids and allocation_review. Link IDs, line-specific purchase references and purchase activity IDs are unique within the selected map. Suppliers are sourced existing entities. Category IDs identify supported calculate-scope-3-category results with a SCOPE3_CATEGORY method record. The entire category is rerun against current activity/factor inputs; its quantities, context and accepted source coverage must reproduce. Native gaps/diagnostics and synthetic assumptions survive. Missing factors retain EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED; synthetic factors require explicit fixture mode, including when mapping previously calculated historical results. Verification results are not appended to GHG state.

The source ID must identify exactly one accepted category component, and purchase_metric_id must equal that component's accepted physical activity ID. Emissions components cannot be reused across mapping rows. Purchase activity uses the shared supported mass, volume, energy or count units; CO2e quantities are explicitly rejected even if a supplied ratio factor makes category arithmetic reproduce. Corporate totals, category aggregate totals, spending proxies and unrelated activities cannot replace this link. Physical activities already reflect buyer allocation; mapping applies no further fraction or factor. Supply separate attributable activity rows for a split purchase rather than duplicating a row.

allocation_review requires confirmed=true, known evidence_ids, supplier_relationship, product_scope, rationale, lifecycle_boundary and allocation_basis. Lifecycle/allocation strings match the accepted component policy exactly. This provides declared applicability, not authenticated supplier identity or source semantics. Purchase references should identify invoice/order lines; distinct physical IDs alone do not prove independence, which remains a source-review obligation.

SUPPLY_CHAIN_MAPPING retains each link, category/status, original purchase and emissions metric records, allocation_reapplied=false and supplier_corporate_emissions_attributed=false. These are referenced historical source quantities, not new calculated metrics; result.metrics remains empty and portfolio_total=null. The supplier register and GHG arrays are unchanged, and procurement_authorized=false. Failed mapping publishes no row register but preserves inherited/native gaps and source history. The helper does not derive lifecycle factors, emissions allocation or supplier corporate-to-product attribution. Broader methods require sourced consuming workflows.

## Supplier engagement and improvement planning

Both helpers reproduce the complete selected score-supplier-sustainability report from SUPPLIER_INPUTS against current state. Altered reports or blocked reproduction reject the entire register. Planning review requires confirmed=true, known nonempty evidence_ids, reviewer_role, rationale, rubric_id, scope_boundary, service_constraints, current reporting period and buyer boundary_id. Rubric/scope/period/boundary match every source assessment. Supplied source-fitness, service and buyer judgments remain unauthenticated. Verification results do not enter history; all original reviews, gaps and assumptions survive.

prioritize-supplier-engagement requires exactly assessment_result_ids, engagement_review, priority_rule and result_id. Select at least one distinct scoring source per distinct supplier. All selected rubrics must have equal criteria/questions, weights, anchors, evidence policies and exclusions. priority_rule is an explicitly ordered nonempty list of distinct {field,direction} entries: unresolved_criterion_count with descending direction and/or weighted_coverage_percent with ascending direction. There are no default fields or weights. This lexicographic rule prioritizes evidence gathering only; it does not estimate impact or use score bounds as measured performance. Unresolved criteria generate the original targeted questions and evidence expectations. Only suppliers with unresolved questions and known selected decision values enter priority groups. Equal values share a group; supplier IDs sort tie display without breaking the tie. Fully supported and all-excluded rubrics have no evidence-request priority. SUPPLIER_ENGAGEMENT retains source scores/ranges separately, questions, groups and supplied review. All requests remain draft_unsent.

develop-supplier-improvement-plan requires exactly assessment_result_id, plan_review, actions and result_id. Each nonempty action register row has exactly id, criterion_id, kind, owner, target_date, description, evidence_target, target_score and depends_on. IDs and criteria are unique within the plan; owners/descriptions/evidence targets are nonempty and dates are canonical ISO dates. data_collection actions target unresolved criteria and require target_score=null. improvement actions target supported buyer_rated criteria; target_score is a declared rubric anchor strictly above the supported current rating. No target is inferred, and excluded criteria cannot become actions. Such targets describe documentation/performance under the buyer's rubric, not guaranteed emissions reduction or supplier acceptance.

depends_on contains distinct known action IDs. Self-dependencies, cycles and prerequisites with later dates reject the plan. The proposed sequence follows prerequisites, then dates/IDs for display; it makes no capacity, critical-path or completion claim. SUPPLIER_IMPROVEMENT_PLAN retains full source criteria and assessments, owned actions and unaddressed unresolved criterion IDs. Proposed collection does not resolve a gap. Every action remains proposed with supplier_agreed=false, implementation_authorized=false and completion_verified=false. delivery_status=draft_unsent, procurement_authorized=false and projected_emissions_reduction=null. Actual supplier agreement, relevant professional/engineering review and procurement decisions are separate gates. Neither helper adds physical metrics, supplier commitments or calculated emissions.

## Selected mapped emissions hotspots

identify-scope-3-hotspots requires exactly mapping_result_ids, hotspot_review, hotspot_threshold_percent and result_id, with optional fixture_mode defaulting to false. Select distinct supported map-supply-chain-emissions results. hotspot_review requires confirmed=true, known nonempty evidence_ids, reviewer_role, rationale, scope_boundary, nonoverlap_assessment, current buyer period/boundary_id and boolean coverage_complete. hotspot_threshold_percent is explicitly supplied, finite, greater than zero and at most 100; no default materiality threshold is assumed.

Every selected mapping reruns through the purchase mapper, including native factor checks and fixture isolation. The full reproduced mapping report must equal its historical report. Native gaps and factor requirements survive a failed check. Mapping and hotspot scopes must match. Across all selected rows, emissions component IDs, purchase activity IDs and purchase-line references must each be unique. All category GWP bases must match; physical/lifecycle/allocation records remain in source maps. Different periods/boundaries and negative gross emissions are rejected. Sourced quantities convert to kg CO2e before summing; no new factors or allocation are derived. Supplier identities and nonoverlap remain supplied review rather than authentication.

The denominator is the sum of distinct accepted mapped components only. Derived common metrics expose the selected subtotal, each supplier and category contribution, and (when the subtotal is positive) 100 × contribution / selected subtotal. Each metric retains current units/period/buyer boundary, evidence, calculation lineage and unquantified source uncertainty. Supplier/category views are alternate partitions of the same denominator and cannot be added to each other or an inventory subtotal. GHG arrays and supplier entities are unchanged.

SUPPLIER_HOTSPOTS preserves selected mapping IDs, supplied review, GWP basis, denominator metric ID/scope, threshold and both contribution views. Contributions sort by point amount; equal amounts share a group, with IDs only ordering tie display. A hotspot meets or exceeds the caller's selected-subtotal percentage threshold. This ordering does not quantify source uncertainty or predict whole-footprint materiality, supplier risk, reduction potential or engagement capacity. Zero subtotals retain valid zero quantities but have null shares/ranks, no hotspot and an explicit gap.

selected_mapping_coverage_complete requires both hotspot confirmation and every mapping's coverage confirmation; a new review cannot erase incomplete upstream coverage. Missing selected coverage creates a gap, and full_scope_3_coverage_verified is always false. This helper does not establish whole-organization inventory completeness. procurement_authorized remains false. See [the fictional replay](../evaluations/sus14-supplier-hotspots.md).

## Supplier adverse-impact risk screening

screen-supplier-sustainability-risk requires exactly supplier_id, screening_review, risk_model, risks and result_id. The supplier is an existing sourced entity. screening_review requires confirmed=true, known nonempty evidence_ids, reviewer_role, rationale, scope_boundary, screening_basis, stakeholder_context, current buyer period/boundary_id and boolean coverage_complete. This declares the selected supplier/product/site context and missing stakeholder assessment; it does not authenticate sources or establish comprehensive due diligence.

risk_model contains exactly id, severity_anchors, likelihood_anchors and priority_rule=severity_groups. Each anchor register has at least two {rank,label,description} entries with distinct positive integer ordinal ranks and nonempty text. Higher severity ranks mean greater severity. These are caller-defined ordinal labels, not OECD numerical scores or probabilities; there are no default country/sector ratings, weights or multiplication.

Each risk row contains exactly id, topic, impact_status, adverse_impact, relationship, product, site, geography, observed_date, period, boundary_id, evidence_ids, evidence_fit, severity_rank, likelihood_rank, scale, scope, irremediability, assessment_rationale, source_applicability, follow_up_owner and follow_up_date. Text fields are nonempty; risk IDs and known evidence references are distinct. Application period/boundary match the current buyer screening, while product/site/geography remain separately declared supplier context. Known observation and follow-up dates are canonical ISO dates, with follow-up not preceding a known observation. observed_date may be null with an explicit gap; access dates cannot replace unknown event dates. Original evidence access dates, source periods/geography, quality and uncertainty are retained in source_records; source_applicability must explain temporal and product/site fit. The helper cannot authenticate that explanation.

topic is environment, human_rights, labour or governance. impact_status is actual, potential or unknown. relationship is caused, contributed, directly_linked or unknown, describing the supplied buyer relationship to an impact, not legal liability. evidence_fit is reviewed_supporting, unverified, proxy or irrelevant. Only reviewed_supporting with nonempty evidence and known actual/potential status yields supported_selected_impact; other records remain investigation_required even with supplied high ranks. Ranks match declared anchors or remain null. Actual impacts require null likelihood; unknown potential likelihood, severity and buyer relationship produce gaps.

SUPPLIER_RISK_SCREENING retains original records, source metadata, supported severity/likelihood separately, unresolved fields and owned follow-ups. Severity groups contain only supported known severities, highest first; equal severity stays tied and likelihood never breaks ties. A supported severity can enter a group while likelihood/relationship remains unresolved, so this is severity triage, not a complete risk assessment. Raw proxy/allegation ranks remain visible but cannot enter supported groups. No row becomes a misconduct finding. An empty register never establishes no risk; incomplete coverage keeps unexamined sites/products/topics unknown.

The helper adds a scoped open PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED obligation for due-diligence/source-fitness review and preserves every previous gate. It creates no physical metrics, aggregate supplier-risk score, legal determination, supplier approval, procurement authorization or contact authorization. Follow-ups remain proposed; supplier disengagement and corrective actions need separate decisions. See [the fictional replay](../evaluations/sus14-supplier-risk.md).

Source orientation checked 2026-10-05: [OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Business Conduct (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1787/15f5f4b3-en), section II 2.3–2.4 and annex Q29–31, distinguishes the enterprise's relationship to actual/potential adverse impacts and discusses severity and likelihood in prioritizing response. Human-rights severity takes precedence over likelihood. This motivates separate fields; the ordinal anchors and severity-group display are an explicit caller model, not a prescribed OECD scoring algorithm, conformity assessment or legal rule.

## Source orientation

Checked on 2026-10-05. [ISO 20400:2017's public abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/63026.html) identifies sustainability integration in procurement as its scope. Only public orientation was consulted; paid clauses were not used, and this workflow does not claim conformity or certification.

[GHG Protocol supplier-data guidance](https://ghgptechassistance.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/37993461124244-How-do-I-collect-primary-data-from-suppliers-and-other-value-chain-partners) supports targeted requests for existing activity records and documentation of methods, sources and assumptions, with attention to capacity and confidentiality. This motivates provenance and questionnaire design; it supplies no universal scoring weights. The helper's score arithmetic is an explicit buyer decision model, not a GHG Protocol or ISO score.

Independent skill behavior, sourced risk methods, broader supplier decision methods, broader attribution methods and organization-wide procurement scenarios remain development/evaluation work. No release readiness is established by packaging or deterministic helper tests.
