# Future-goal proposal evidence

`assess-future-goal-claim-evidence` adds the draft `claim-future-goal-0.1.0` source bridge and `claim-evidence-0.5.0` assessment report. It follows reviewed revision 7fff776 and needs separate scoped review. The common envelope contains the eight bound-material parameters plus `goal_review`; run it through `python -m scripts.run_claims request.json`. Existing claim operations and their historical outputs retain their behavior.

`goal_review` has exact fields `transition_result_id`, `roadmap_result_id`, `pathway_id`, `target_result_id`, `target_period`, `target_year_span`, `as_of_date`, `boundary_id`, `period`, `subject_id`, `evidence_ids`, `evidence_fit`, `interpretation`, `rationale`, `reviewer_role`. Interpretation is `proposed_target`, `committed_target`, `guaranteed_achievement` or `unknown`; evidence fitness is supporting, unverified or irrelevant using the existing review labels. These are attributed observations, not authenticated expertise or owner adoption.

The exact proposed transition, implementation roadmap and developed target are replayed from their retained `STRATEGY_INPUTS`. Their complete reports, metrics/uncertainty and evidence references must match current state. The roadmap must reference the selected transition; the selected pathway and its final checkpoint must reference the selected target and reproduce the same target snapshot. Missing/blocked/unrelated sources or changed reports, metrics and lineage block with `CLAIM_FUTURE_GOAL_REQUIRED`; any unresolved factor remains `EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED`. No proposed metric becomes observed performance.

The review keeps reporting/evidence period, representation dates and planned target period separate. The numerical claim requires the selected target's actual endpoint metric and exact unit. A future endpoint is eligible only as proposed target evidence in this operation; its future period is not relabeled as the current evidence period. `target_year_span` binds a nonempty Unicode character span inside the exact original statement to the annual target period. Different years, truncated larger year/date/range tokens or selectors outside the statement block with `CLAIM_MATERIAL_REQUIRED`. Initial support is for explicit annual year labels; subperiods, multi-year ranges and finer deadline semantics require further methods and qualified interpretation.

Source fitness additionally requires a matching organization/boundary/reporting period, explicit supporting evidence and current version/access metadata, an unchanged source as-of date matching the claim review, and a live plan/roadmap review window. Only a supplied proposed-target interpretation is eligible. A proposed plan supplies no commitment, guarantee or owner authority. Synthetic sources/materials cannot enter real-use mode. Whole-organization and facility/product claims remain unfit for this selected-pathway bridge.

The quantity criterion must select the developed target; plan criteria must select the transition or roadmap. Both can produce qualified proposal evidence when the original qualifications and supplied visibility are suitable. An actual original 7 kWh/count statement against a reproduced proposed 8 kWh/count endpoint can be potentially misleading without a legal finding. Intensity units are preserved: a proposed intensity endpoint supplies no absolute emissions reduction, total inventory or net-zero claim.

Progress always remains insufficient in this initial bridge. Proposed monitoring milestones, modeled checkpoints, responsibility acceptance, resource estimates and planning dates are not measurements of delivered performance. The report retains monitoring proposals and unmet/unresolved delivery conditions, with `observed_progress=null`, verification/adoption/guarantee/funding/implementation/absolute-emission/publication flags false. A missing monitoring proposal remains visible and is not invented. A future-goal claim therefore cannot become fully substantiated through this operation alone.

`CLAIM_FUTURE_GOAL_SOURCE_CHECK` contains the single complete source bridge and `CLAIM_FUTURE_GOAL_INPUTS` retains exact parameters. Criterion rows reference that same diagnostic by source-result ID and SHA-256 of its UTF-8 message. Original sources, factors, history, gaps, assumptions and reviews survive the checked proposal. The helper creates no metric or stronger statement and resolves no review.

The [fictional evidence](../evaluations/sus20-future-goal-claim.md) demonstrates proposed endpoint/plan inspection, missing progress, conflicting originals, guarantee withholding and deadline binding. Independent semantic/visibility/source review, actual progress/commitment/adoption/funding evidence, target feasibility and counterfactual science, actual jurisdiction/source/rights decisions, independent organization evaluation, individual skill interfaces, routing/skillsets and release readiness remain open. This increment does not close SUS-20 or the full roadmap goal.
