## Initial helper interface

`python -m scripts.run_finance request.json` accepts the common envelope. `run_finance(state, skill, parameters)` returns a common result and checked append-only proposal; exit 0 includes blocked results, exit 2 is invalid request. Financial calculations enter result history without inventing a project-register entry. Output periods describe the economic horizon; input reporting context describes the valuation review. No state writes, investment approval or external action occur.

All operations require analysis_review and result_id plus the inputs below. analysis_review has confirmed=true, currency (three-letter code), definition, rationale, model_assumption, valuation_date, horizon={start,end}, dollar_basis=real/nominal, tax_basis=pre_tax/after_tax and evidence_ids. Valuation date lies in the state's reporting period; horizon starts on that date and ends later. Code shape is not currency-registry verification. Supplied economic definitions and completeness assumptions need actual review; field presence is not authentication. Output assumptions and unquantified uncertainty remain explicit. Outstanding reviews/gaps survive, including gaps that do not change arithmetic. Composition outputs use their source reference periods; valuation measures use the declared study horizon.

Inputs resolve to finite sourced metrics at the current organizational boundary. Monetary units match the declared currency exactly; annual savings use currency/year. Future/recurring benefits need an explicit metric assumption and linked tier 5 model evidence. Supply already-supported net quantities; the initial helper does not derive tax, inflation, operating costs or avoided physical use. Separate real cash flows with real rates from nominal cash flows with nominal rates. No missing flow is filled with zero.

### Simple payback

calculate-simple-payback inputs: investment_id and annual_savings_id. Positive investment is in the analysis reporting period. The reviewed definition_code is investment_over_constant_annual_net_savings. annual_savings_period exactly matches a full-year reference metric. Net annual savings must be positive and explicitly assumed constant for the scenario. Payback = investment / annual net savings in years, without discounting. It does not account for lifetime replacement costs, varying benefits or capital recovery beyond the supplied horizon. Beyond-horizon arithmetic is flagged; no recovery within the study horizon is established. Nonpositive net savings blocks a finite positive payback.

### Initial project-cost composition

calculate-sustainability-project-cost accepts lines, composition_review, analysis_review and result_id. This initial helper composes initial_net_investment only, not a lifetime cost model. Lines have exactly id, metric_id, kind=cost/credit and category=capital/installation/fees/initial_tax/contingency/capital_incentive. Capital incentives are credits; other categories are costs. Recorded magnitudes are nonnegative and match declared currency, reporting period and organizational boundary. Their signed sum retains a net credit, which cannot serve as positive investment for ordinary simple payback.

composition_review requires confirmed=true, basis=initial_net_investment, boolean coverage_complete, measurement_boundary, nonoverlap_assessment, allocation_basis, tax_treatment, rationale and evidence_ids. Optional coverage_details maps selected metrics to nonoverlapping source fragments under the common baseline contract. Inspect quotes/invoices and incentive eligibility before confirming; the helper authenticates neither eligibility nor tax/allocation judgments. It rejects duplicate quantities and selected total/component lineage overlap. Shared documents require distinct fragments. Initial costs cannot include operating benefits, lifetime replacements or recurring charges simply relabeled as capital. Incomplete coverage yields a qualified subtotal and gap. No FX, tax adjustment, capital contingency percentage or incentive amount is inferred.

### Projected operating-savings composition

calculate-operating-savings accepts baseline_lines, scenario_lines, comparison_review, analysis_review and result_id. Lines have the same id/metric_id/kind/category shape, with category=energy/water/material/waste/maintenance/labor/fixed/other/rebate. Rebates are credits and other categories are costs. All quantities have units currency/year, nonnegative magnitudes and exactly the same reviewed full-year reference period. Their annual nature must already be sourced; partial records cannot be annualized by changing labels. This helper supports projected comparisons; observed-difference composition remains a separate future method.

comparison_review requires confirmed=true, analysis_type=projection, comparison_basis=same_service/adjusted_baseline, annual_period={start,end}, boolean coverage_complete, baseline_conditions, scenario_conditions, adjustment_method, nonoverlap_assessment, fixed_cost_treatment, rationale and evidence_ids. model_evidence_ids must identify known tier 5 records with explicit assumptions. Optional baseline_coverage_details and scenario_coverage_details reconcile each register separately. Supply a documented adjusted baseline rather than asking the helper to invent weather/service coefficients or tariffs. Economic dollar/tax basis is the supplied analysis assessment and must be supported by source review.

The result is baseline net annual operating cost minus projected scenario net annual operating cost. Include changed maintenance, retained fixed charges and supported rebates before calling it net savings. An unchanged fixed-charge metric may occur in both alternatives and cancels; duplication within either alternative is rejected. Nonoverlap controls and source-fragment reconciliation operate within each register. The helper cannot infer hidden interactions, unchanged services or omitted future costs from review prose. Negative savings is retained. Incomplete coverage yields a subtotal with a gap, which survives downstream payback. Projected output links the supplied model evidence and assumptions; no realized or verified saving is established.

### Horizon ROI

calculate-roi inputs: investment_id and net_benefit_id. definition_code=horizon_net_gain_over_investment. The numerator is a supplied undiscounted net horizon gain AFTER all costs including the initial investment, and its metric period equals the economic horizon. ROI = net gain / positive investment * 100. Retain negative ROI. Do not subtract investment twice, call total gross benefits net gain, annualize the output, or equate it with IRR. The helper cannot authenticate that the numerator includes every applicable cost; inspect its composition before confirming.

### Annual end-of-year NPV

calculate-npv inputs: cashflows (one {year,metric_id} for every year 0 through N) and discount_rate_id. The supported timing is calendar_year_end, with December 31 valuation and a horizon ending December 31 of year N. Year 0 metric uses the analysis reporting context and represents the net amount at valuation time; subsequent annual net cash-flow metrics have the corresponding full-calendar-year periods. Rate is an explicit percent metric in the analysis reporting context, greater than -100%; discount_basis must match dollar_basis. Zero rate is valid. NPV = sum of cashflow_t / (1 + rate/100)^t, including signed year 0. Retain negative outcomes.

Every future annual net flow is supplied explicitly, with projected evidence/assumptions. Reusing one metric across years, duplicate/missing year slots and selected aggregate/component lineage overlap are rejected. Each year is one already-composed net flow, not a pile of invoice lines; source composition and hidden overlap need review. No midyear, irregular-date, fractional-period or monthly convention is inferred. Additional timing methods remain development work.

### Annual IRR and ambiguous roots

calculate-irr accepts cashflows, root_search, analysis_review and result_id. It uses the same complete, signed calendar-year-end cash-flow/timing/horizon and source checks as NPV, without a supplied discount-rate metric. root_search contains exactly lower_percent, upper_percent, rate_tolerance_percent, npv_tolerance (in the declared currency) and max_iterations. Both finite bounds exceed -100%, lower < upper. Tolerances are positive; rate tolerance is at most 0.000001 percentage points and NPV tolerance at most 1e-8 of the absolute cash-flow sum. Iteration budget is 1..1000. The numerical method supports 1..60 future years. These are solver limits, not recommended economic search bounds or discount rates. Extremely near-singular bounds fail if the supported precision cannot distinguish them from -100%.

The helper transforms x=1/(1+r) and searches the positive bounded interval for zeros of sum(CF_t*x^t). Recursive derivative roots partition monotonic intervals; bisection refines crossings and tight internal roundoff checks retain tangent candidates. It uses 70-digit Decimal arithmetic for root isolation and verifies NPV residuals in the supplied currency. The serialized JSON rate must also meet rate and NPV tolerances; iteration exhaustion or unrepresentable precision blocks the measure rather than emitting an unverified result.

IRR_ROOT_SEARCH records candidates, residuals, search settings and cash-flow sign changes. Only one verified bounded candidate with exactly one nonzero-coefficient sign change emits a single percent metric: the positive-x polynomial sign rule establishes at most one rate above -100%. Negative and zero IRR are retained. Multiple candidates emit IRR_MULTIPLE_ROOTS with no scalar IRR. One bounded candidate with multiple sign changes emits IRR_GLOBAL_UNIQUENESS_UNPROVEN without selecting a rate; other candidates may exist outside the supplied bounds. No bounded candidate gives IRR_ROOT_NOT_FOUND and does not prove global absence. All-zero flows give IRR_INDETERMINATE, because every admissible rate closes NPV. These outcomes retain diagnostics/gaps/review obligations as valid blocked analyses.

Search candidates are numerical, not formal exact root certificates for arbitrary ill-conditioned polynomials. Tight verification can withhold a result. Root precision does not quantify economic uncertainty or completeness. NIST Handbook 135 section 6.3 distinguishes ordinary IRR from AIRR and discusses multiple-return concerns; this helper computes ordinary IRR, without inventing a reinvestment rate or presenting it as AIRR/MIRR. Irregular-date IRR, formal global certification for nonconventional flows and alternative return measures remain further methods.

### Annual cash-flow composition

The initial build-sustainability-business-case helper accepts lines, cashflow_review, analysis_review and result_id and emits one signed net annual flow. It supports the same December 31 valuation, calendar_year_end timing and December 31 horizon as NPV/IRR. cashflow_review requires confirmed=true, integer year=0..60 within the horizon, boolean coverage_complete, timing_basis, nonoverlap_assessment, constant_annual_assumption, rationale and known evidence_ids. Future years also require model_evidence_ids identifying tier 5 evidence with assumptions. These recorded judgments are not independent authentication of the supplied financial basis or completeness.

Each line has exactly id, metric_id, kind=inflow/outflow/signed and basis=dated_amount/constant_annual. Inflow/outflow amounts are nonnegative magnitudes; outflow is subtracted. Signed net amounts retain negative and zero values. Dated amounts use the declared currency and selected year's full-calendar period, or the state reporting period for valuation year 0. Their payment date within that source period is explicitly modeled at year end. constant_annual uses currency/year with an exact reviewed annual_reference_period covering a full year, and is allowed only in future years. It schedules one annual amount under the explicit constant model; it does not infer inflation, escalation, partial-year prorating or cash timing from billing records. Repeating that sourced annual model in separately identified future-year outputs is allowed, with its common source lineage and modeling assumption retained.

The nonempty line register rejects duplicated metric/line IDs and selected aggregate/component overlap. Optional coverage_details reconciles distinct source fragments under the common baseline contract, after source periods have been checked and explicitly scheduled into the selected year. Do not add gross savings alongside the already-composed net saving. Missing amounts or years are not automatically zero; an explicit sourced zero can be scheduled. An incomplete register yields a subtotal and FINANCIAL_COVERAGE_REQUIRED, preserved downstream. Existing unrelated gaps and review obligations remain open.

Call once for each year and pass the resulting distinct currency metrics as the complete NPV/IRR cashflows list. Initial positive investment becomes an outflow; operating net savings uses signed kind. Replacement costs and residual credits require separately sourced dated amounts in their corresponding years. The helper does not infer their absence, asset lifetime, tax treatment, terminal value or eligibility. The saved fictional composition case has flows (-1000,500,500) CAD, NPV -132.23140495867767 CAD at supplied 10%, and verified IRR approximately 0%. It is an author-led arithmetic replay, not independent business-case evaluation. Full alternatives, sensitivity, ownership and decision composition remain further work.

Supply exactly one {year,quantity_id,price_id,price_context} for each future year 1..N of the December 31 calendar_year_end horizon, with N=1..60. No missing year is silently filled and no single metric is repeated across years. Each output is a separate currency metric result_id-year-N covering that calendar year, with quantity/rate calculation lineage. No lifetime sum, discounting or escalation is inferred.

scenario_review requires confirmed=true, boolean coverage_complete, quantity_unit, quantity_basis, service_boundary, nonoverlap_assessment, excluded_charges, escalation_basis, rationale, known evidence_ids and model_evidence_ids referencing tier 5 records with assumptions. The quantity must itself have projected evidence/assumptions. The annual rate may be a directly sourced future price rather than a model, but must have evidence. Nonnegative finite quantities and rates, including explicit zero, are accepted. Unknown quantities, negative prices and rebates require reconciliation or separate methods. Inputs share the organizational boundary and exact selected annual period. Physical units and rate denominators match exactly: energy kWh/MWh/MJ/GJ, resource kg/t/L/m3/count, carbon kg CO2e/t CO2e; price uses currency/physical-unit. Convert explicitly upstream when needed; no fuel heating value, density, mass/volume conversion or FX is invented here.

Each price_context requires charge_type=variable_unit, effective_period matching the selected full-calendar year, dollar_basis and tax_basis matching analysis_review, and nonempty rate_version, service and applicability descriptions. The annual result is quantity * rate for the selected variable-unit charge only. Fixed, demand, time-of-use, taxes, rebates and procurement/inventory interactions need separate sourced models; the helper does not turn reduced usage into avoided fixed charges or avoided purchases. Coverage_complete is supplied review of the selected exposure, not proof that omitted charges are zero or total project savings are established. Carbon review also requires exposure_basis=internal_shadow/regulatory_payment/contractual_charge, applicability_basis, eligible_coverage, exclusions and reviewer_role. This operation never derives emissions or emission factors; use the sourced GHG workflow first, preserving EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED where derivation lacks a factor. All carbon outputs add an open PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED obligation for applicability and exclusions. Internal shadow pricing adds NONCASH_SHADOW_PRICE: its monetary value is analytical and cannot be consumed as a payment/cash-flow/return by finance helpers, including through derived metric ancestry. Payment scenarios remain conditional and preserve qualified review; analytical completion establishes neither a legal obligation nor eligibility.

Separate energy, resource, carbon-shadow and conditional carbon-payment proposals are replayed in full. ### Project cost per positive abatement

calculate-marginal-abatement-cost accepts incremental_cost_id, baseline_emissions_id, scenario_emissions_id, abatement_review, analysis_review and result_id. Its initial method is a selected-project net-cost-per-abatement ratio, not a derivative of an economy-wide cost curve or an independently established market marginal price. [IPCC AR6 WGIII Figure SPM.7](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/figures/summary-for-policymakers/figure-spm-7/), checked 2026-10-05, provides context for net lifetime monetary costs and baseline-relative mitigation; this helper supplies no IPCC costs or discount-rate defaults and makes its own supported project convention explicit.

The numerator is already-supported project-minus-baseline net cost in the declared currency, covering exactly analysis_review.horizon. The two nonnegative gross physical emissions metrics cover exactly that same horizon and organizational boundary and use the same explicit kg CO2e or t CO2e unit. All three need projected assumptions and tier 5 model evidence. No annual cost can be divided by lifetime tonnes, and no unit, GWP, boundary or period restatement is inferred. Abatement = baseline emissions - scenario emissions, undiscounted; it must be strictly positive. The ratio is reviewed net cost / abatement, in currency/CO2e mass. Negative and zero costs remain valid; negative abatement is not labeled cheap mitigation. A second output records the physical abatement with lineage to both source emissions quantities.

abatement_review requires confirmed=true, boolean coverage_complete, abatement_type=physical_reduction, offsets_excluded=true, emissions_basis=undiscounted_horizon_total, emissions_unit, incremental_cost_definition=project_minus_baseline_net_cost, cost_basis=undiscounted_horizon_net_cost/discounted_horizon_net_cost, cost_sign_convention=net_cost/negative_net_cashflow, scope_boundary, baseline_conditions, scenario_conditions, functional_service, nonoverlap_assessment, exclusions, method_comparability, reviewer_role, rationale and known evidence_ids. Offsets and negative gross emissions/removals need separate methods. The baseline/scenario service, coverage, additionality and hidden interactions require substantive review; field presence cannot authenticate them. Incomplete declared coverage gives a conditional selected-project ratio with a gap, not a complete cost curve.

source_contexts maps exactly the three selected metric IDs to confirmations of value, unit, period, boundary_id, method and evidence_ids. Both emissions confirmations additionally supply identical nonempty gwp_basis and the declared scope_boundary. Confirmations are checked against current metrics; calculations do not invent or transform GWP values. This arithmetic consumes supplied defensible emissions rather than deriving them from activities. An EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED diagnostic anywhere in selected emissions metric ancestry withholds the ratio and propagates that code and data need. A directly supported external emissions assessment need not be rederived, but its provenance, assumptions and applicability remain review obligations.

Already-discounted cost requires an explicit discount_rate_id resolving a sourced percent metric in the state reporting period, greater than -100%, and discount_basis matching the declared real/nominal basis. The helper records that convention; it does not discount a supplied numerator again or certify that an external assessment applied the rate correctly. If the numerator is a calculate-npv output, its saved economic basis must match currency, valuation date, horizon, dollar/tax basis and discount rate. Its net-cashflow sign must be explicitly inverted with negative_net_cashflow before treating it as net cost. NPV cannot be relabeled undiscounted. For other source quantities, the explicit source review must support the supplied net-cost interpretation.

All supported outputs add an open PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED obligation for cost completeness, baseline/scenario applicability, physical reduction and exclusions. Existing reviews and factor/engineering gaps remain. Internal shadow-price monetary ancestry remains unavailable as a cash-cost numerator under the finance resolver. No certified abatement, offset eligibility, funding approval or emissions claim results from this arithmetic.

The saved fictional horizon case uses 300 CAD net incremental cost and emissions of 100 versus 90 t CO2e, producing 30 CAD/t CO2e and 10 t CO2e reduction. A -200 CAD net cost gives -20 CAD/t CO2e. Zero/negative abatement is withheld. Tests also compose supplied NPV into the ratio with explicit sign inversion and review the inherited source context. These are author-led numerical cases; authentic engineering/cost/GHG assessments and independent behavioral evaluation remain further work.

### Comparable project register

compare-sustainability-projects accepts projects, criteria, comparison_review, analysis_review and result_id. Supply at least two alternatives and a nonempty criterion register. Each criterion has exactly id, unit, direction=maximize/minimize, period_basis=horizon/reporting_period and definition. Definitions must keep unlike measures distinct: NPV, ROI, IRR, payback, physical reduction and risk/feasibility measures are separate columns, not automatically interchangeable numbers. Source methods must match within each column; the initial helper withholds unlike methods instead of inventing a restatement.

comparison_review requires confirmed=true, boolean coverage_complete, baseline_id, service_basis, comparability_basis, rationale, known evidence_ids and source_contexts. Every selected metric is confirmed once with value, unit, period, boundary_id, method, evidence_ids and the exact criterion definition. CO2e columns also require matching nonempty gwp_basis and scope_boundary. Supply compatible supported quantities before comparing; no currency, period, physical-unit, GWP or boundary conversion occurs. Horizon metrics require model assumptions and tier 5 evidence. An EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED diagnostic in selected metric ancestry withholds comparison.

Each project has exactly id, owner (named role or null), readiness=ready_for_screening/deferred, constraints (nonempty description), dependencies (each {description,satisfied}), interacts_with (selected alternative IDs), evidence_ids, economic_basis, baseline_id, service_basis and metrics. economic_basis exactly confirms the common analysis currency, valuation_date, horizon, dollar_basis and tax_basis; baseline/service identifiers match comparison_review. Each criterion's metrics entry is {metric_id,range}, with range={low,high,unit,basis,evidence_ids}. Bounds must be finite, ordered, use the criterion unit and contain its estimate. These are supplied scenario envelopes, not inferred probability or confidence intervals. The common input period applies to reporting-period criteria and the declared horizon to horizon criteria.

Supported finance outcomes must have a reproducible FINANCIAL_ANALYSIS_BASIS. The helper reruns each selected source calculation and compares its metric contents against current inputs, checking the common economic basis. NPV columns also require comparison_review.discount_rate_id matching each source's explicit rate. For abatement-cost outputs, the comparison's GWP/scope declaration must also match the saved baseline-emissions source context and abatement review, preventing a caller from relabeling incompatible physical bases. Recycled comparison/rank outputs cannot become original project measures. External supported measurements use explicit source confirmations; this does not independently authenticate source documents, feasibility or hidden interactions.

Interactions are declared symmetrically. Reused outcome quantities require interacting alternatives. The result contains separate original measure columns linked to their source metrics, and PROJECT_COMPARISON records the register, definitions, source reviews, ranges and deferral reasons. Null owner, deferred readiness and unsatisfied prerequisites make an alternative ineligible for subsequent screening rank, while retaining its row. No project registry entry is invented and portfolio_total remains null. Incomplete coverage qualifies the comparison with a gap; all existing gaps/reviews persist.

### Explicit investment screening ranks

Thresholds are in their declared criterion units and must be ordered. Estimates failing a supplied threshold are deferred with a reason; constraints, unassigned owners and unsatisfied prerequisites remain visible. Exact criterion tuples tie with competition ranks (1,1,3); IDs only stabilize display. A reversed owner-supplied priority order can reverse the screening result. Eligible rank metrics have unit rank and lineage to the comparison's separate measures. Deferred rows retain null rank. No eligible alternative gives a blocked result while preserving the diagnostic register and reasons. Overlapping primary scenario ranges produce explicit sensitivity findings: estimate ordering is not established as robust across those supplied scenarios. This screening does not propagate probability, claim formal dominance, prove ranking robustness, or optimize a joint portfolio. Thresholds apply to estimates rather than guaranteeing feasibility across every scenario. Weighted scoring and broader sensitivity/portfolio methods remain further work.

INVESTMENT_RANKING includes priorities, thresholds, rows, ties/deferrals, ranges, sensitivity and a null portfolio total. All open professional/engineering reviews and coverage gaps survive. It does not make or record an approved funding decision.

case_review requires confirmed=true, boolean coverage_complete, rationale, claim_boundary, reviewer_role, known evidence_ids, problem, decision, alternatives and source_contexts. problem has statement, baseline_id, service_basis and known evidence_ids; baseline/service must match the ranking's original comparison. decision has exactly owner, requested_action and status=pending_human_review. Owner/action are nonempty role/description strings. This status cannot be changed to approved by analytical composition. The result adds an open PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED obligation for financial/physical assumptions, sensitivity, implementation feasibility and decision/claims boundaries.

It is rerun and checked with the same currency, valuation date, horizon and real/nominal/tax basis. Its recorded cash-flow and discount inputs remain visible in the case. Explicit scenarios do not prove exhaustive sensitivity, probabilities or robust ranking, and their physical consequences are not inferred from financial inputs.
