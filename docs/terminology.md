# Shared terminology

**Evidence**: a traceable source record, including explicitly labeled proxies. **Provenance**: source and transformation lineage for an analytical output. **Methodology**: a named, versioned basis for analysis. **Boundary**: included/excluded organizations, facilities and activities plus the consolidation approach. **Period**: explicit start/end dates; calendar and fiscal labels alone are insufficient.

**Activity data**: measured or estimated operational quantity. **Emission factor**: sourced emissions per activity unit with geography, vintage and method. **CO2e**: greenhouse-gas quantity on a stated global warming potential basis. **Scope 1/2/3**: inventory classifications determined under the selected methodology; classification is not derived from a label alone.

**Baseline**: a documented reference state with period, boundary and methodology. **Restatement**: a traceable revision retaining the prior baseline. **Uncertainty**: limits on interpretation, including explicitly unquantified uncertainty. **Data gap**: a missing, conflicting or unsuitable input plus impact and remedy. **Review requirement**: an unresolved professional decision obligation distinct from execution status. **Adapter**: a versioned mapping around core capability. **Skillset**: a composed workflow using named atomic skills.
