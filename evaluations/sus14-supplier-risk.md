# Fictional supplier adverse-impact screening

Author-led deterministic scenario; no real allegation, independent source authentication or legal determination is established. [The full capture](sus14-supplier-risk.json) retains the original state, exact request and complete checked proposal.

A fictional packaging supplier has a selected site spill report and a separate general sector labour concern. The buyer supplies a three-anchor ordinal model and describes scale, scope, irremediability and applicability. The reviewed site-specific actual impact carries severity rank 2 and null occurrence likelihood. The sector concern carries raw rank 3 but remains proxy evidence with unknown buyer relationship; it never becomes supported supplier harm or an overall risk score.

The severity queue contains only the supported spill. Both records retain owned follow-ups and source metadata. Incomplete coverage and unknown buyer relationship remain gaps, and a new due-diligence review obligation stays open. Supplier entities, history and prior gates survive. No misconduct finding, procurement/supplier approval, contact authorization or legal determination is made.

Checks cover missing/unverified/irrelevant evidence, tied severities, unknown potential likelihood, empty screening, unknown severity, invalid anchors/dates/periods/ratings, duplicate IDs and source-text integrity. Full output replays exactly. Independent behavior and broader assessment/response methods remain open.

A separate [independent raw-source scenario](sus14-independent-risk.json) withheld severity because extent/remediation were insufficient and retained the labour note as a proxy. Its first run exposed the missing unknown observation-date representation; the corrected rerun preserved null plus a gap, separately from access date. [Before](sus14-independent-risk-before.md) and [after](sus14-independent-risk-after.md) reports preserve actual source judgments and execution evidence. The complete corrected proposal reproduced exactly in a parent rerun. Geographic metadata contradictions remain review needs. This single scenario does not establish broad behavior or source authentication.
