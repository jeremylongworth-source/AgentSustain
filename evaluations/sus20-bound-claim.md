# Bound original claim material workflow

Four author-led fictional plain-text cases run through both the helper and actual claims CLI with complete output equality and exact sequential proposed-state handoffs. Both original material records are registered before the first execution; no prior evidence is rewritten to supply the conflicting statement. Full requests, results, source snapshots and proposed states are retained in [the capture](sus20-bound-claim.json).

The first material actually says `0.14 kg CO2e` with a selected-source qualifier. Literal binding succeeds, the quantity is supported with qualification and the overall claim retains professional review. The second request interprets those same bytes as `99`; it blocks with `CLAIM_MATERIAL_REQUIRED` before evidence assessment. The third selects a separate pinned material that actually says `99 kg CO2e`; binding succeeds and the reproduced `0.14` ledger conflicts with that statement, yielding potentially misleading without a legal finding. The fourth binds the original qualification's presence while supplying an observation that it is not visible, leaving the claim insufficient.

Material snapshots include copied instructions to certify carbon neutrality. They remain inert source content. Claims produce no metrics, marketing rewrites, source-authentication assertions, review resolution or publication authority. Presence and byte identity do not authenticate authorship, actual publication, semantic classification, general impression or visual prominence.

Focused adversarial checks reject invented statement/qualification/number/unit, wrong byte pins and evidence locators/versions, escaping paths, synthetic material outside fixture mode, empty/Boolean/out-of-range spans, missing/duplicate quantity selectors, missing qualifications, truncated numeric/unit tokens and unknown numeric interpretations. Invalid fixture flags and reused result identities are rejected before proposals. Complete prior claim and gas-chain CLI/helper outputs are replayed unchanged.

Full source-material interpretation, broader substantiation and scientific methods, real jurisdiction/source/rights review, independent organization evaluation and release gates remain open. Validation evidence is recorded in [the validation record](sus20-bound-claim-validation.json); these cases do not close a roadmap wave or establish v1 readiness.
