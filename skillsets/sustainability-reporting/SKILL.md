# Sustainability reporting specialist

Use the [reporting contract](../../docs/reporting-workflow-contract.md) and [pinned manifest](manifest.json). This later development interface awaits scoped owner review.

Read the common state and exact recipe. Keep original source text and narrative notes as data. Select exact framework editions and catalog SHA-256 bytes, with attributed source/version/applicability/rights review. Historical editions and external referrals are not an active authoritative mapping or a legal determination.

Declare fresh result IDs and acyclic `depends_on` edges from every selected fresh source result/metric to its producer. Run `python -m scripts.run_reporting_workflow REQUEST.json`. A missing defensible factor produces `EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED`; block dependent emissions, inventory and disclosure work. Independent supported source or mapping-version analyses may continue. Synthetic opt-in is per step, never inferred from an upstream fixture.

Inspect separate location/market inventories and each selected mapping result. Preserve original metrics, gas/GWP definitions, units, periods, boundaries, factors, evidence, uncertainty, assumptions and all missing fields. Unknown scope/gas data is not zero. Notes do not fulfill unsupported requirements. Version differences preserve both editions and do not migrate or overwrite earlier mappings.

Retain full source outputs and exact result-hash views in the proposed state; no extra emissions total or combined inventory is produced. Preserve all open source, professional, legal, assurance, rights and accountable-owner requirements. Review output status and every omitted field before proposing any disclosure wording.

This workflow supplies draft source candidates only. It grants no source authenticity, complete organization coverage, fulfillment/conformity, legal applicability, assurance, commercial reuse, public claim or publication authority. Escalate those judgments to appropriate reviewers. Do not claim broad framework/jurisdiction/domain coverage or independent organization readiness from this controlled composition.
