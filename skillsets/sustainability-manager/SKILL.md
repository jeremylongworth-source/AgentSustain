# Sustainability manager

Use the [organization workflow contract](../../docs/manager-workflow-contract.md) and [draft dependency inventory](manifest.json). This subsequent interface awaits scoped owner review; dependency approval does not activate this manager or establish independent organization reliability.

Inspect the common source state, organizational boundary, reporting period, evidence, measurement/service definitions, factors, assumptions, uncertainty and open reviews. The recipe starts with already normalized business records; it does not read raw bills, authenticate records or supply missing data. Preserve unsupported sources and factors as gaps, including `EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED`. Keep physical observations, monetary models and future target/roadmap periods distinct.

Invoke `run_manager(state, parameters)` or `python -m scripts.run_manager request.json` with `skill=sustainability-manager`. The selected strategy and operational energy baselines must use the same raw metric IDs; KPI definitions, proposed targets, strategy, transition, roadmap and disclosure must bind fresh recipe sources. Select an exact fresh inventory for disclosure and retain its unknown scopes/categories. The runner uses existing helpers without changing their methods. A blocked prerequisite stops its dependent stage; independent physical, financial, target or mapping branches may continue when their own selected sources remain supported.

Review each source result and its units, method, period, boundary, uncertainty and evidence through the report's exact result references and hashes. A 20% intensity proposal supplies no 20% absolute carbon reduction or verified delivery. Monetary returns are model outputs whose operational benefit attribution remains unverified. Work packages, owners and milestone dates remain proposed; missing resource/approval evidence is not zero demand or permission to start. Historical framework/source mappings remain qualified candidates with source, rights and applicability review open.

Return the common aggregate and one atomic proposed revision, retaining all intermediate results, source/domain/opportunity records, gaps and review obligations. Produce no combined cross-domain total or stronger public claim. The manager registers open professional and assurance reviews, resolves none, grants no funding/implementation/conformity/publication authority and writes no shared-state file. Report coverage and ingestion/evaluation limits; specialist/router, independent end-to-end organization and release requirements remain open.
