# Sustainable procurement

Use the [procurement workflow contract](../../docs/procurement-workflow-contract.md) and [draft dependency inventory](manifest.json). This subsequent specialist awaits scoped owner review and establishes no production activation or independent supplier/organization reliability.

Inspect supplier identity, buyer rubric, physical purchase lines, functional service, lifecycle/GWP basis, period/boundary, source evidence and open reviews. Supplier ratings and allegations are supplied observations; unknown ratings remain ranges/gaps, not zero performance or authenticated impacts. For mapped emissions use explicit separately supplied factors and the existing carbon/category methods. Missing defensible factors stay `EMISSION_FACTOR_REQUIRED`; no corporate total or spend proxy substitutes for attributable physical purchase activity.

Invoke `run_procurement(state, parameters)` or `python -m scripts.run_procurement_workflow request.json` with `skill=sustainable-procurement`. Select bounded existing supplier/carbon operations with fresh IDs and explicit acyclic prerequisites. Produced result and metric sources use separate namespaces and name their producer as an ancestor. Blocked prerequisites stop dependent invocation; independent analyses may continue. Every synthetic carbon/mapping/option step opts into fixture mode individually.

Inspect retained source outputs for questionnaire/response gaps, anchored buyer ratings, comparison bounds/ties, evidence priorities, owned improvement proposals, risk uncertainty, purchase mapping/hotspots and conditional equivalent-service options. Keep those outputs separate. Do not add mutually exclusive alternatives to an inventory, convert rubric scores to emissions, treat an allegation as verified harm or interpret a modeled option difference as an implemented reduction. Retain cost scope, service/lifetime/lifecycle constraints and missing supplier coverage.

Return exact result references/hashes and the common aggregate with one atomic candidate revision. All source results, units, methods, periods, boundaries, uncertainty, evidence, assumptions, gaps and review obligations survive. The specialist emits no combined portfolio total or selected supplier, resolves no review, sends no questionnaire/message, places no order and grants no procurement/implementation/publication authority. Obtain actual buyer/source/professional decisions separately. Independent behavioral/organization evaluation, specialist/router approval and public-v1/release requirements remain open.
