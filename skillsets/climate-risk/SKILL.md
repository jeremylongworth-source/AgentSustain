# Climate risk

Use the [climate workflow contract](../../docs/climate-workflow-contract.md) and [draft dependency inventory](manifest.json). This subsequent interface awaits scoped owner review; it is not an active router registration or proof of independent organization reliability.

Inspect source population, locations/assets, reporting period, scenario, horizon, hazard/model basis, exposure and vulnerability definitions, ordinal/range assumptions, transition source editions and open review requirements. A mapped source is not verified local exposure; a transition driver is not an actual obligation or business effect. Preserve unsupported populations, proxies, missing drivers, dates, causal pathways and uncertainty rather than inventing probability, loss or safety.

Invoke `run_climate_workflow(state, parameters)` or `python -m scripts.run_climate_workflow request.json` with `skill=climate-risk`. Select the fourteen existing climate operations with fresh IDs and explicit acyclic prerequisites. Produced result sources name their producer as an ancestor; actual metric ownership is checked separately. Blocked prerequisites stop dependent invocation, while independent physical or transition branches may continue when their own sources remain supported. The runner changes no underlying climate method or source register.

Review exact source results and preserved scenario/horizon/period context through their IDs and hashes. Do not add ordinal ratings across risks or turn ordinal bounds into confidence intervals, exceedance probabilities, monetary damages, legal requirements or verified adaptation benefits. Resilience actions retain prerequisites, constraints, side effects, future acceptance evidence and owners as proposals. Source fitness and actual implementation still require qualified site/domain/owner review.

Return the common aggregate and one atomic candidate revision with all source results, evidence, metrics/uncertainty, boundaries, assumptions, gaps and existing reviews retained. The specialist emits no portfolio risk total, resolves no review and declares no organization safe, actual legal obligation, validated model, proven effectiveness, implementation or publication approval. It writes no source/state file and triggers no external action. Independent behavioral/organization evaluation, wider climate methods, specialist/router approval and public-v1 requirements remain open.
