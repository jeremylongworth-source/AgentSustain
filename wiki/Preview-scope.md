# Preview and repository scope

The public development repository contains reusable skills, seven specialist entry points, Python helpers, shared schemas, fictional examples and recorded validation. Coverage includes data/evidence, supplied-factor carbon calculations, energy, water, waste/materials, business cases, procurement, strategy, climate risk and qualified reporting proposals.

The limited helper preview is a separate, smaller package: selected original data, energy, water, waste/material and financial helpers, shared schemas, a fictional conversion and documentation. It excludes skills/skillsets, routing catalogs, standards/jurisdiction research, historical captures, private archives and real emission/GWP factor databases. A prepared archive is not a published release; follow its README and distribution notice.

In the public repository, real GHG Protocol catalogs and Canadian jurisdiction source packs are omitted following the source/history remediation. Framework demonstrations use original fictional catalogs and a TNFD external-standard referral. Requests for omitted real-source packs return `SOURCE_PACK_REQUIRED`; no fictional rule is substituted for Canadian law or an issued framework.

The food-manufacturer example uses a synthetic electricity factor. Its subtotal is partial, with unknown refrigerant and other unresolved emissions sources. It is not a complete real-company footprint. Synthetic factors are for explicit fictional exercises only.

The full SUS-00 through SUS-25 [roadmap](https://github.com/jeremylongworth-source/AgentSustain/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) retains source-backed methods, framework/jurisdiction work, organization acceptance and professional boundaries. Omission from this edition does not complete or abandon that work. See [source notices](https://github.com/jeremylongworth-source/AgentSustain/blob/main/standards/SOURCE_NOTICES.md) and [domain boundaries](https://github.com/jeremylongworth-source/AgentSustain/blob/main/docs/domain-contract.md).
