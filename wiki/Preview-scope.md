# Preview scope

The proposed preview includes selected original data, energy, water, waste/material and financial helpers, shared schemas, a fictional conversion example and these wiki pages. It supports inspection and narrow arithmetic experiments. It is not the entire AgentSustain skill library or a production organization workflow.

The preview excludes all standards/framework/jurisdiction catalogs and research, emission/GWP factors, private source archives, historical captures, skillsets/skills and routing catalogs. No Canadian compliance, framework conformity, emissions inventory, professional advice or installed-agent behavior is promised by this package. Excluded capabilities remain in the full development roadmap; they have not been abandoned or approved for distribution.

The full repository has controlled organization-workflow evidence, including a two-facility fictional food manufacturer. Its electricity subtotal uses a synthetic factor and its unresolved sources stay partial or blocked. That result is not a complete or real company footprint. Preview scope is intentionally separate from full v1 acceptance.
