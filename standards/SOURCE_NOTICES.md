Original AgentSustain code and documentation are covered by the project MIT license. That license does not relicense external publications or data. These notices identify observed sources and changes; they do not accept new terms, grant permission, activate a module or clear a publication hold.

The [GHG Protocol website terms](https://ghgprotocol.org/terms-use) include public/commercial content-use restrictions. The [Canada.ca terms](https://www.canada.ca/en/transparency/terms.html) distinguish noncommercial reproduction, commercial permission and third-party material. These observations are not a determination that original factual references infringe, nor that access supplies a distribution license.

Scientific/legal/source fitness and source encoding/currentness are separate from distribution rights. Existing catalog/source hashes, inactive/draft flags and review requirements remain unchanged. No source publication, publisher software, logo, real emissions-factor database or private raw-source archive is newly bundled by this notice.
